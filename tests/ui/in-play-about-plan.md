## Concrete blockers and minimum further cases

**Local versus remote Grapple/projectile isolation:** the current launcher supplies one
room, but the fixture currently controls only one connected player per context. A bot
is not proof of a remote player's ownership. Preparing this combined case needs a second
real client in the same room, both identities/rosters settled before retaining observer
hooks, and normal controls to attach both cords and launch a remote projectile. Neither
private cord insertion nor calling a send/launch method proves this. Proposed separate
bounded slice: native attach on both peers, record actual owner identities and projectile,
open About on one, assert its cord disappears while the peer's cord/projectile persists
through the first normal step, then fresh local attach positive. Recipe/range/flight-time
preconditions require source review before adding that peer flow. Current patch does not
claim even the combined case's local half passed.

**Delayed pointer-lock completion:** headless input exposes a real request but no reviewed
way to deterministically delay native fulfillment. Replacing requestPointerLock with a
controllable promise would be synthetic promise-ordering evidence only. Minimum native
case needs a controlled permission/fulfillment mechanism; a separate explicitly synthetic
race test could supplement it after agreement, but cannot be labelled native proof.

**Real visibility:** calling PauseState hidden or dispatching visibilitychange is synthetic.
The existing checks remain labelled that way. A native test needs verified document.hidden
transitions from actual browser foreground/background control, then modal open/close and
user-paused variants with observed focus and pause reasons. Headless tab switching is not
assumed to provide those transitions. Physical-device visibility remains open.

No observer or product fix is proposed without a concrete failing result and separate
permission. These gaps are not discharged by the previously passing four input slices,
by the original two About contexts, or by these prepared extensions.
