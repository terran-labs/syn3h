# Earlier tank studies

These are source snapshots, not new versions of the designs. Each round retains its own
procedural kit so changes to the current tank cannot silently change an earlier study.
They appear under **Mira tank: earlier studies** in the vehicle lab.

The earliest committed generator is round 2. Its successor explicitly calls the previous
Bastion design round two. No round 1 generator appears in the repository's tank history;
round 1 is therefore labelled **source not recovered**, not permanently lost.
The tank lead also checked its reflog, stashes and local capture artifacts without finding it.
These snapshots do not reconstruct that missing source. Rounds 4 onward stay
with the tank lane's current exhibit. The jet already retains rounds 1 through 6, the buggy
retains rounds 1 through 4, and the hovercraft has its first round.

The archived materials intentionally reproduce the original study. These modules are lab
code and are not imported by the game or included in its production entry.
