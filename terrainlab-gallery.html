Syn3h Terrain Lab: island prototype

Every world is one island. A breached volcano with a lava lake stands at the centre, a lava river runs from the breach to the sea, players spawn on a ring near the coast facing inward, and lava runs to the horizon. Prototype only: this is not in the game yet.

Open the live lab (drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, type a seed and press Build). Add &night=1 or &liquid=water to the address.
