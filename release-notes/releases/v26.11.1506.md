## Syn3h v26.11.1506

Explore by default, clearer phone controls and generated impact sounds.

Public release at 1:30pm Pacific on 15 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

The second afternoon train makes Explore the default while preserving compatibility with the frozen judging builds. It combines tested interface, lobby and sound improvements. Typecheck and the final 1,030-test gate passed, with one unchanged competitive bot-flee failure. The combined build also passed mixed-client driving and mode checks against frozen versions 1503 and 1504. Both public addresses passed exact-build, multiplayer and frozen-file checks, and all eight Chrome/WebKit vehicle cases passed.

### Features

- New rooms start in Explore, with Tag game and King of the Hill available by choice. Landmark races are opt-in.
- A floating thumb stick appears when you drag on the left side of the game on a touch screen, follows your thumb and fades when released.
- Vehicle contacts use generated hull sounds. Landings and grounded impacts use the terrain material beneath the vehicle.

### Fixes

- The lobby starts closer on portrait phones, with tighter orbit spacing and compact population counts shown only when players are online.
- Low-quality lobby tilt finishes settling so an idle view can stop drawing.
- Opening the buggy wings silences the motor modulation, including when the wheels remain on the ground. Road noise and the wing hum remain.
- Normal play imports only the approved generated Sherpa tank; design studies stay in the vehicle lab.
- Repeat vehicle switches retain the cache improvement shipped in v26.11.1505. This release also warms the actual cached vehicles after startup. In the lane's final headless Chrome run, all 20 switches compiled no new graphics programs and the longest frame was 153 ms, including first uses. Warming itself produced a 704 ms frame after startup; it moves the work away from selection rather than eliminating it.
- Default bots continue following even in Tag game. Competitive fleeing and hunting require aggression above 1.5 or following to be disabled.
- Grapple uses generated light tethers, moving beads, a star-shaped hook and pulsing anchors, with a reduced-detail version at the lowest graphics level.
- Shared engine particles stop inside Zorb.

### Play

[Play this exact release](https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1506). The plain address serves the latest accepted release.

- Drag on the left to drive on a phone or tablet.
- Choose Tag game or King of the Hill when you want a competitive round.

### Known issues

- Frozen builds share the live room server. Their older interface may mislabel Explore, but their driving and mode changes remain compatible.
- The materialization hold and its local tag protection are not included in this release.
- One competitive bot-flee test still fails exactly as on the baseline. The default following checks and browser regrouping checks pass.
- Buggy handling changes remain excluded because their combined tests introduce new failures.
- Bright water-surface glow and underwater cloud overlay remain pending.
- Touch gestures were checked in automated WebKit runs; physical-device feel remains unmeasured.
- The showcase animation is the earlier project teaser, retained because this release's fresh capture was incomplete.
