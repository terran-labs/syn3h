## Syn3h v26.11.1802

Vehicles float on the river you see, rivers are level from bank to bank, and the waterline sits where the water meets the lens.

Public release at 6:19pm Pacific on 18 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

The water you saw and the water vehicles floated on did not always agree. On rivers the game floated vehicles on a surface of its own, up to many metres from the river drawn on screen, so a buggy could ride above the water or sit sunk in a shallow stream. Now physics floats things on the river that is drawn, the river is level across its width, and the line across the camera at the surface is where the water truly meets the lens.

### Features

No new features in this release.

### Fixes

- Vehicles float on the river you see, not above it.
- Rivers are level from bank to bank.
- The line across the camera at the water's surface sits where the water meets the lens.
- A buggy in a shallow river is no longer treated as sunk.

### Handling

- Vehicles on rivers now ride the drawn river's surface, so their height on a river changes where the old surface sat above or below it.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/, drive into a river, and watch the vehicle sit on the water you see; dip the camera to the surface to see the waterline.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1802, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Not measured for this release.

### Known issues

- On phones at Low and Medium, lakes and rivers are drawn flatter than the waves vehicles ride, by up to about a quarter of a metre.
- At one river bend into a pool, the drawn water and the floating surface still differ at a few points.
- How the chat card and the keyboard behave on real phones is not yet confirmed.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
