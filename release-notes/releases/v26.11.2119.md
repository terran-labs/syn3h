## Syn3h v26.11.2119

Brighter Plasma Tag strikes, a visible cord by day, smoother water.

Public release at 9:05pm Pacific on 21 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

A handful of polish. Plasma Tag strikes flash where they leave and stay visible on a distant vehicle. The Grapple's cord now shows against a bright sky. The first dive under water no longer stalls the game, and neither does a quality step near waterfalls or a wake. Bots' and other players' hovercraft and jets now throw headlight beams in mist, like buggies already did. And in the lobby, planet names fade where they would run into the buttons or the Settings card.

### Features

- Plasma Tag strikes flash where they leave and stay a visible size on a distant vehicle.
- Bots' and other players' hovercraft and jets show headlight beams in mist at High and above.

### Fixes

- The Grapple's cord is visible by day; at night it looks as before.
- The first dive under water no longer stalls the game.
- Quality steps no longer rebuild a waterfall's mist or a vehicle's wake.
- In the lobby, planet names fade where they would touch the buttons, the Settings card or the version number.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/, pick an Archipelago world, dive under the sea, and try the Grapple and Plasma Tag in daylight.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.2119, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

The first dive, the waterfall mist, the wake and the new beams build no shaders in play on either graphics path in our checks with four bots.

### Known issues

- A player still on a version before v26.11.2118 who pauses is treated as hidden, so the host role passes after 3 seconds instead of 20.
- At Liquid the gear selector is shown as glass pills; at the other looks it is bare names.
- Liquid has not been timed on any phone.
- Entering a world at Ultra or Cinematic, the first automatic drop can still hitch.
- On the older graphics path, choosing a top quality level before pressing Play makes entering a world slower.
- On the older graphics path, changing vehicle at Low can cause a small hitch.
- The in-game performance check that found this has not yet been run for the jet and the buggy at their own detail-switch distances.
- No phone has been measured for these quality steps.
- The sun rays' GPU cost was not measured, and whether they flicker while driving was judged by eye only.
- Changing the quality level in play rebuilds the firework batches.
- A player still on the previous version sees Plasma Thunder as it was and ignores the new messages. Their version never reports where its bolts end, so players on this version burst those bolts on their own after 1.5 s, and those bursts push nobody.
- The first touch on a phone builds one effect pipeline.
- On WebGL 2, preparation spends about 3 s waiting between completion checks.
- Not measured on a physical iPhone; touch was emulated on a Mac.
- Phone behaviour was tested in browser engines on a Mac, not on a physical iPhone.
- A player on an older version whose cord holds someone cannot be unhooked by the new stop words; stretching the cord still frees them.
- At dawn, fog lies on the sea along coasts.
- On dry ground, a vehicle left on its roof still rights itself after a few seconds; whether it should is the owner's to decide.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
