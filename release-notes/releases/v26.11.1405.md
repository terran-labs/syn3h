### The story so far

King of the Hill went in: the room server runs the rules, the clock and the scores, the zone is placed on the summit from the terrain's own data, and a glowing marker shows where it is. Under the rules of this build, a vehicle that reached lava or fell off the world was taken out of play and returned to its pad; that changed later in the day. Buggies learned to float on lava and water, bots were tuned to chase the Syn3h buggy along roads and paddle out of lava, and a tank was ported but kept reachable only by its address. The owner also opened a separate Terrain Lab workspace to direct the island generator directly, and its first island prototype appeared in this stretch.
