## Syn3h v26.11.1700

Water that sinks an idle buggy, bots on first-name terms, a calmer lobby, and Y for chat whatever the cursor.

Public release at 2:13am Pacific on 17 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

A buggy in water used to glide on as if on ice: with no throttle it kept going for over a hundred metres. Now water holds it up only while it works to stay up; let go and it slows hard and settles on the bed, where it can still drive. Bots now go by a single first name, as people do. The lobby is calmer and closer: a planet's caption brightens when you point at it, idle planets breathe, the hint text is gone, and the view eases back home after you look around. Y opens and closes chat even while the game holds the cursor.

### Features

- Water: a buggy stays up only while it works to, with throttle or speed. With no throttle it slows hard, goes under and sinks to the bed, where it drives along the bottom. Brakes bite in water, and opening the wings still floats it up. Lava, ice, the hovercraft, the jet and the Zorb are unchanged. Three new Water settings: Sink, Lift speed and Coast.
- Bots go by a single first name everywhere: labels, the Players card, the minimap, chat and toasts. Each keeps its own voice in chat, and its job is in what it says, not in its name.
- Lobby: a planet's caption eases to full strength when you point at it. The "Drag to look around. Tap a world to go there." hint is gone (screen readers still hear it). When the lobby is idle each planet breathes its own glow at its own slow pace. The default view is closer, with the Syn3h name about half the screen wide, and after you drag or zoom the view eases back over about 25 seconds.
- Chat: Y opens chat in any play state and interface mode, with the cursor still captured and the caret in the field. Enter on an empty line, Escape or Close leave chat, and Y closes it when the field is not in use.

### Fixes

- King of the Hill: holding the hill scores on the room's own clock, once a second, so it no longer depends on how smoothly a player's page is running. A holder on a struggling device keeps scoring, and keeps the hill through a short stall.
- Y did not open chat while the game held the cursor, and in the Hidden interface the panel could open invisible.
- Escape that only leaves the chat field no longer resets the interface mode.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ in a browser. Point at a planet to read its name, then tap a world. Drive into a lake and let go of the throttle; open the wings to come back up. Press Y to say hello to the bots by name.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1700, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Not measured for this release.

### Known issues

- Players on an older build in the same room still see the old long names for the same bots.
- Planets between the three old sizes look wrong to players on frozen older versions.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
- Touch aiming was tested with simulated touches in a phone-sized browser, not yet on a physical phone.
