## Syn3h v26.11.1906

Bots play with you in Explore, and bump each other too.

Public release at 10:00pm Pacific on 19 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

Bots in Explore now and then want to play. A bot may offer you a tow or start a group tug, and it says so first. If you would rather they did not, say stop and they will leave you alone for the rest of the session. Bots also bump each other now, so in Tag the role can pass from bot to bot and on the hill one bot can knock another off. And the hovercraft and the jet now throw headlight beams through mist, as the buggy does.

### Features

- In Explore, a bot may now and then offer a tow or a group tug, saying so first. Say stop and they will, for the rest of the session; say you can play again to bring it back.
- Break free of a cord in Explore with your own Grapple or a double tap of brake.
- New setting: Bots may play with me.
- Bots now bump each other too: in Tag the role can pass between bots, and on the hill a bot can knock another bot off.
- Your hovercraft and jet now show headlight beams in mist.

### Fixes

No other fixes in this release.

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/, start a room with bots in Explore and wait for one to offer a tow. Then try Tag and King of the Hill with several bots, and drive the hovercraft at dawn.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1906, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Not measured for this release.

### Known issues

- A player on an older version whose cord holds someone cannot be unhooked by the new stop words; stretching the cord still frees them.
- An occasional long frame after a bot arrives at Low is being looked at.
- At dawn, fog lies on the sea along coasts.
- Bots' vehicles do not show headlight beams in mist yet.
- On dry ground, a vehicle left on its roof still rights itself after a few seconds; whether it should is the owner's to decide.
- How the chat card and the keyboard behave on real phones is not yet confirmed.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
