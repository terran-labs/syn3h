## Syn3h v26.11.1907

Bots come and go without freezing the game.

Public release at 11:00pm Pacific on 19 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

The game used to stall for a moment whenever a bot joined, came back or left, because it was building the shaders that draw that vehicle and its effects in the middle of play. Now every vehicle's and effect's shaders are prepared behind the loading overlay, before the world appears. A bot joining, returning or leaving no longer builds shaders, and neither does a second or third bot of the same vehicle. Loading into a world takes longer as a result. We have not measured how much longer reliably, so there is no figure yet.

### Features

- New: Performance, Diagnostics, Copy diagnostics copies a private report of your device, startup timings, shader counts and any visible frame stalls. Nothing is sent anywhere; you choose where to paste it.

### Fixes

- Every vehicle's and effect's shaders are now prepared behind the loading overlay before the world appears.
- A bot joining, returning or leaving no longer builds shaders, including repeats of the same vehicle.
- Bots share the drawing work they can while keeping their own paint, wheel glow and sparks.
- A name given in a link now shows on your own vehicle and in the Me card.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/, start a room with several bots and play while they come and go. Then open Performance, Diagnostics and choose Copy diagnostics.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1907, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Loading into a world takes longer, because the shaders are prepared before it appears; the extra time has not been measured reliably. On the tested desktop, bots joining, rejoining and leaving built no shaders on either graphics backend.

### Known issues

- Changing the quality level still rebuilds some shaders.
- On WebGL 2, a second buggy seen at medium distance can still cause a short stall.
- Entering a world again repeats the preparation.
- The first touch on a phone can build one effect shader.
- A player on an older version whose cord holds someone cannot be unhooked by the new stop words; stretching the cord still frees them.
- At dawn, fog lies on the sea along coasts.
- Bots' vehicles do not show headlight beams in mist yet.
- On dry ground, a vehicle left on its roof still rights itself after a few seconds; whether it should is the owner's to decide.
- How the chat card and the keyboard behave on real phones is not yet confirmed.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
