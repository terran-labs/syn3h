### The story so far

The shores stayed wild on the owner's word: guaranteed beaches round every spawn pad were built and then reverted, and force field wings were chosen as the way out of steep coasts and lava instead. The lava sea stopped burning to white at night once the sky's auto exposure was corrected at its cause, the chase camera learned to stay above lava and sea at a shore, and a two-player ram check passed 30 times out of 30. The owner reported that the Syn3h buggy barely turned at cruising speed, so that fix, and one for buggies wrongly treated as upside down after a ram, went on top of m13 as m13.1. At 6:25pm the owner asked for the domain to be switched over as soon as possible; once Cloudflare access arrived, this release went live at 7:08pm and www.syn3h.com was pointed at it at 7:13pm.

### Features

- Drive the Syn3h buggy with force field wings, the classic buggy, a hovercraft or a jet, each with your own body and accent colours, saved on your device
- Explore a generated volcanic island with a lava lake in its crater and a spiral road to the summit, or choose a desert or alpine world from the lobby; every world is built in your browser from a shared seed
- Play with friends in Tag or King of the Hill, with tag beams, and join a room from an invite link or a QR code
- Add bots that drive, chase and contest the hill alongside players
- Change gravity, sky and physics with sliders while you play; world settings are shared by everyone in the room
- The Syn3h buggy now turns at cruising speed as readily as the classic buggy

### Fixes

- A buggy that is rammed or landed on while upright no longer sags and pulls away slowly; it is only treated as upside down when it is
- Lava stays orange at night instead of burning to white, and its crust keeps fine detail up close
- The chase camera stays above the lava and the sea at a shore
- The jet's nozzle glow and other hot effects keep their colour at night
- Buggy wheels no longer race while paddling in water

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ in a browser, choose a vehicle and press Play. Press H in game for every control.

- Buggies: W A S D or the arrow keys to drive, Shift to brake, Space for force field wings, L or click for a tag beam
- Jet: R to lift off and climb, F to come down, W and S to tip the nose, A and D to bank, Space to switch between hover and flight
- Hovercraft: W for thrust, A and D to turn, Shift to brake
- Camera: C cycles chase, cockpit, orbit, stationary and roam
- On a phone or tablet: the left thumb drives and steers, with WINGS, BRAKE and TAG buttons, or THR + and THR - on the jet

### Known issues

- The jet could show a black 3D view in Safari when one of its textures was still downloading. v26.11.1413 fixed it.
- On a parked jet, W alone does nothing; R lifts off, and the controls card did not say so plainly
- Rear-ending a moving car passes the tag but gives the car in front no shove on its own screen
- Bots reach every summit, then ram each other, and some drive off the edge
- Joining a room saved by an older build could give a mixed world; start a fresh room name
- Changing vehicle in the lobby reloads the page
- Link previews of the game show no image
