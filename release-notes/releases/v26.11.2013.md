## Syn3h v26.11.2013

The first automatic quality step down from Medium no longer hitches.

Public release at 11:40pm Pacific on 20 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

The last release stopped the hitch on the first automatic step down from High. This one does the same when you entered at Medium and the game first steps down to Low: the sun and its shadows stay in the scene, and at Low the shadow work is simply switched off rather than rebuilt, so that first step builds nothing in play. A second drop later in the same session can still hitch.

### Features

No new features in this release.

### Fixes

- The first automatic quality step down from the level you entered at, Medium to Low, no longer hitches.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ at Medium, enter a world, then switch Performance quality to Low and back.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.2013, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

The first step down from Medium to Low builds no shaders on either graphics backend. At 3840 by 2160, Low looks the same as before: identical on WebGL 2, and 19 of 8.3 million pixels differ by one step on WebGPU. Low's GPU time did not rise; no speedup is claimed. Time from Play to playable is within noise of the previous release.

### Known issues

- A second automatic drop in the same session, such as Medium to Low after High to Medium, can still hitch; a fix is in progress.
- No phone has been measured for these quality steps.
- Stepping from Low to the lowest level builds 9 shaders, and 17 on the way back.
- Changing vehicle at Low builds up to 7 shaders.
- Larger manual jumps, such as High to Low, still hitch; a short veil to cover them is being built.
- The experimental glass shader has not been timed on any phone. Merging buttons and the press effect are pilots on one pair of buttons.
- The sun rays' GPU cost was not measured, and whether they flicker while driving was judged by eye only.
- Changing the quality level in play rebuilds the firework batches.
- A player still on the previous version sees Plasma Thunder as it was and ignores the new messages. Their version never reports where its bolts end, so players on this version burst those bolts on their own after 1.5 s, and those bursts push nobody.
- The first touch on a phone builds one effect pipeline.
- On WebGL 2, preparation spends about 3 s waiting between completion checks.
- Not measured on a physical iPhone; touch was emulated on a Mac.
- The button glass is drawn with CSS; the experimental glass shader is off by default.
- Phone behaviour was tested in browser engines on a Mac, not on a physical iPhone.
- A player on an older version whose cord holds someone cannot be unhooked by the new stop words; stretching the cord still frees them.
- At dawn, fog lies on the sea along coasts.
- Bots' vehicles do not show headlight beams in mist yet.
- On dry ground, a vehicle left on its roof still rights itself after a few seconds; whether it should is the owner's to decide.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
