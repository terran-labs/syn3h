## Syn3h v26.11.1903

Bots arrive with far less shader work on desktop.

Public release at 5:00am Pacific on 19 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

Every vehicle used to build its own copy of the materials for its paint and lights, so each bot that arrived stalled the game while its shaders were built. Now bots and other players of the same vehicle kind share their materials, and a bot's arrival builds only a fraction of the shaders it did. This is a desktop gain; phones draw vehicles more simply and are unchanged.

### Features

No new features in this release.

### Fixes

- Bots and other players of the same vehicle kind now share their materials, so a bot's arrival builds a fraction of the shaders it did (at High, about a tenth). This is a desktop gain; phones are unchanged.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ on a desktop at High, start a room with bots, and watch them arrive.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1903, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

At High on WebGPU, a bot's own shader builds on arrival fell from 67 to 179 ms to 5 to 41 ms.

### Known issues

- An occasional long frame after a bot arrives at Low is not fixed by this and is being looked at.
- At dawn, fog lies on the sea along coasts.
- Other vehicles do not have headlight beams yet.
- On dry ground, a vehicle left on its roof still rights itself after a few seconds; whether it should is the owner's to decide.
- How the chat card and the keyboard behave on real phones is not yet confirmed.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
