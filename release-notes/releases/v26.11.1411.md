### The story so far

At about 5:15pm the owner set a rule for every world: no violence, and every vehicle stays in play. Within half an hour the game followed it: a vehicle in lava is now stuck and glowing instead of removed, it can crawl out, and it is lifted back to its pad if it is still stuck when the delay ends, so King of the Hill no longer takes anyone out of play. The beams were renamed tag beams in every label, and a test now scans every readable string in the game for words of harm. A bot test on the island found bots sitting still on their pads with the throttle open after being returned: the buggy's self righting read a contact count that is always zero against the terrain's triangle mesh, so an upside down flag was never cleared and the suspension stayed off. It was fixed, and returning a vehicle to its pad now resets its state. The same stretch brought volcanic, desert and alpine planets from one generator, and a lobby restyled to the game's original art direction, drawn with a shader and one vector path instead of images.

### Features

- Sticky lava: vehicles stay in play, glow while cooling, can crawl out, and return to their pad
- King of the Hill in which nobody is taken out of play
- Tag beams, and no harmful words anywhere a player can read
- Volcanic, desert and alpine planets from one generator, chosen by the address or a share link; the lobby's planet cards followed in v26.11.1412
- A restyled lobby with a generated starfield and a vector logo, and the build label shown
- A world reset with a two press confirm, and a gulp for lava and a splash for water

### Fixes

- A buggy returned to its pad no longer sits still on its skid plate with its wheels spinning
- Bots route across the island's roads and keep their identity when recovered
- A firmer Syn3h buggy suspension with a bump stop and a ride height floor
- Livelier hovercraft glide defaults, chosen by the owner
