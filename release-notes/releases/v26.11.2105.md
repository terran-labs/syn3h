## Syn3h v26.11.2105

Fewer quality-drop hitches, and far hovercraft arrivals no longer hitch.

Public release at 2:42am Pacific on 21 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

Earlier releases stopped the hitch on the first automatic quality drop in a session, but a second drop could still stall the game. Now, when you enter a world at High or below, every lower quality level is prepared behind the loading cover, so in a room with no other vehicles the game can step down as many times as it needs without building anything in play. With bots or other players present, each drop still builds shaders for their vehicles. A hovercraft arriving from far away no longer hitches either. The cost is a slightly longer load.

### Features

No new features in this release.

### Fixes

- In a room with no other vehicles, repeated automatic quality drops no longer hitch when you enter at High or below.
- A hovercraft arriving from far away no longer hitches.
- The buggy's motor sparks are prepared for the lowest quality level too.
- In the experimental glass, which remains off by default, a firework behind the gear list no longer makes its labels hard to read.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ at High, start a room with bots, and play until the game steps its quality down more than once.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.2105, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Loading from Play to playable is about 12 ms longer on the newer graphics path (WebGPU) and about 0.46 s longer on the older one (WebGL 2), measured over five pairs before two small follow-up fixes. In return, in an empty room, 20 of 20 real automatic drops, entering at High and at Medium on both graphics paths at desktop and phone size, built nothing in play, and 72 of 72 first arrivals of three vehicle kinds at six distances built nothing for their arrival.

### Known issues

- With bots or other players present, each automatic quality drop still builds shaders for their vehicles and can hitch for up to about a second; a fix is in progress.
- Entering a world at Ultra or Cinematic, the first automatic drop can still hitch.
- The in-game performance check that found this has not yet been run for the jet and the buggy at their own detail-switch distances.
- No phone has been measured for these quality steps.
- On the older graphics path (WebGL 2), changing vehicle at Low still builds a few shaders.
- The experimental glass shader has not been timed on any phone. Merging buttons and the press effect are pilots on one pair of buttons.
- The sun rays' GPU cost was not measured, and whether they flicker while driving was judged by eye only.
- Changing the quality level in play rebuilds the firework batches.
- A player still on the previous version sees Plasma Thunder as it was and ignores the new messages. Their version never reports where its bolts end, so players on this version burst those bolts on their own after 1.5 s, and those bursts push nobody.
- The first touch on a phone builds one effect pipeline.
- On WebGL 2, preparation spends about 3 s waiting between completion checks.
- Not measured on a physical iPhone; touch was emulated on a Mac.
- The button glass is drawn with CSS; the experimental glass shader is off by default.
- Phone behaviour was tested in browser engines on a Mac, not on a physical iPhone.
- A player on an older version whose cord holds someone cannot be unhooked by the new stop words; stretching the cord still frees them.
- At dawn, fog lies on the sea along coasts.
- Bots' vehicles do not show headlight beams in mist yet.
- On dry ground, a vehicle left on its roof still rights itself after a few seconds; whether it should is the owner's to decide.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
