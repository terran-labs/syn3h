## Syn3h v26.11.2000

One glass look, phone panels, chat bubbles and a pause screen.

Public release at 1:02am Pacific on 20 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

This release is about how the game looks and feels around the world rather than in it. Every button now shares one glass style, in the lobby and in play. Phones get a way to open the panels one at a time. Chat reads as bubbles, and pausing now clears the screen and offers a screenshot. The glass is an imitation built in CSS; a closer match to Apple's is in progress. Phone behaviour was tested in browser engines on a Mac, not on a physical iPhone.

### Features

- Every button now shares one glass style, in the lobby and in play.
- When paused, the interface fades away and offers three buttons: Exit world, Welcome and Screenshot. Press S to save a picture at your screen's full resolution.
- On phones, a Show panels control sits above Tag and Wings. Panels open one at a time at full width, with Close panels at the bottom.
- Chat messages are single bubbles, with the name in the player's colour and a robot mark for bots.

### Fixes

- Lobby panels open without a box around them: the planets fade and the logo rises.
- The Syn3h name fades in with the planets, and about half a second after landing when you return to the lobby.
- The Tag button no longer keeps a leftover highlight.
- On phones with a home indicator, the thumb buttons and the minimap sit 10 px lower.
- In portrait, the readouts are plain text.
- The idle line is plain text and steps aside for messages.
- A sunk buggy now pulses the Zorb control instead of telling you to open wings.
- Your own "it" marker in Tag stays clear of the centre of your view.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ on a phone and on a desktop. Open the panels, send a chat message, play Tag, then pause and press S.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.2000, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Not measured for this release.

### Known issues

- The glass is a CSS imitation; a closer match to Apple's is in progress.
- Phone behaviour was tested in browser engines on a Mac, not on a physical iPhone.
- A player on an older version whose cord holds someone cannot be unhooked by the new stop words; stretching the cord still frees them.
- An occasional long frame after a bot arrives is being worked on.
- At dawn, fog lies on the sea along coasts.
- Bots' vehicles do not show headlight beams in mist yet.
- On dry ground, a vehicle left on its roof still rights itself after a few seconds; whether it should is the owner's to decide.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
