## Syn3h v26.11.1709

A black-and-grey interface, an altitude readout with your choice of units, and brighter planet names in the lobby.

Public release at 3:00pm Pacific on 17 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

These are design changes, shipped so the owner can adjust them on the live game rather than in a preview. The interface loses its blue tint: panels, plates and the sidebar are now near-black glass with grey lines and white lettering, and anything switched on shows in plain white. The driving readouts now show altitude above the sea, and one Units setting chooses metric or imperial for every distance and speed. In the lobby, planet names are brighter and take on their planet's glow when you point at them.

### Features

- ALT replaces "M ABOVE GROUND": the readout shows altitude above the sea surface at rest, or above height zero on worlds without a sea.
- A Units setting chooses metric or imperial for speed, altitude, race distance and the bungee cord readout. It starts from your browser's region (US browsers get mph and feet), and a saved choice wins.
- The speed and altitude readouts sit evenly either side of their arcs at every screen size.
- Lobby planet names are brighter and glow in their planet's colour when you point at them.

### Fixes

- The interface's panels, sidebar, Me card, About and Updates are black and grey glass instead of blue, with neutral white for anything switched on.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/, point at the planets in the lobby, then open the sidebar in a world to see the new black-and-grey panels.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1709, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Not measured for this release.

### Known issues

- These design changes are shipped for the owner to adjust on the live game; expect further changes to colours and layout.
- On a phone held sideways, the jet's throttle-down button overlaps the navigation buttons. The fix is on the touch controls branch.
- On an iPad, the navigation buttons overlap the King of the Hill winner announcement.
- The touch stick and thumb buttons keep their blue fill for now.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
