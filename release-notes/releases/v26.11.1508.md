## Syn3h v26.11.1508

Six quality levels, a Performance card and clearer water.

Public release at 2:00pm Pacific on 15 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

This release starts from accepted v26.11.1506 and adds the tested quality controls, meadow and Water fixes. Version 1507 was briefly deployed, failed public acceptance and was withdrawn. Its new materialization hold and gear changes are excluded here while separate diagnosis continues. Typecheck and all 940 tests in the changed folders pass. Both public addresses passed exact-build, multiplayer and frozen-file checks, and all eight Chrome/WebKit vehicle cases passed.

### Features

- Choose among six graphics levels from Minimal to Cinematic in the Performance card, or let Auto adjust quality. Individual settings can be changed and reset.
- Vegetation bends around vehicles and follows the wind. Night scenes gain generated drifting lights, and each world has plant controls.

### Fixes

- WebGL starts at Medium or below and automatic increases stop at Medium. Players can explicitly choose higher levels.
- Post effects follow the quality tier and effect strengths. The separate single-pass renderer is not included.
- Underwater cloud overlays fade with the camera blend. The distant sea ring now faces upward, removing the broad underside reflection seen from below.
- Phone quality controls have a full touch target and readable setting labels.

### Play

[Play this exact release](https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1508). The plain address serves the latest accepted release.

- Open Performance to choose a level or turn Auto on.
- Explore a meadow or look below the water surface to see the world changes.

### Known issues

- Version 1507's frozen link is unavailable after its rollback; its number will not be reused.
- Materialization hold, Jet settling and the withdrawn gear changes await separate validation. Repeat-switch caching and first-use warming from 1506 remain.
- The competitive bot-flee test failure and the Buggy handling issues disclosed in 1506 remain. No new handling patch is included.
- Four release-history fixture checks remain stale and fail identically on accepted 1506; the new withdrawn-version check passes.
- Physical-device frame-rate limits remain unmeasured. Manual high settings can exceed a device's comfortable performance.
