## Syn3h v26.11.1905

Headlights stay on in first person, arrivals glow, and a softer sea.

Public release at 8:27pm Pacific on 19 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

Seven fixes, including the three from v26.11.1904, which was withdrawn before it went live. The far sea no longer shows a hard line where deep water begins, the water switch says what it does, and a lost graphics device no longer raises an error on the page. Switching into first person used to lose your road lights along with the vehicle model; now the beams stay. A vehicle's arrival used to light the scene with a point light, which lit up nearby bodywork and cost shader work whenever quality changed; now it draws a glow that follows the ground in the vehicle's accent colour. And the arrival effects and the volcanic night motes are prepared behind the entry overlay, so their first appearance does not stall the game.

### Features

No new features in this release.

### Fixes

- The far sea no longer shows a hard line where deep water begins.
- The water switch is now called "Current water (off: classic look)", so it says what it does.
- A lost graphics device no longer raises an error on the page before the Reload notice appears.
- In first person your road lights stay on when the vehicle model is hidden, and swapping vehicles at night keeps the beams and your headlight setting.
- On desktop at High and above, an arrival now glows on the ground in the arriving vehicle's accent colour. Nearby vehicle bodywork no longer catches light from an arrival.
- Changing the quality level no longer adds or removes an arrival light.
- Volcanic night motes and the arrival cloud, beam, ground glow and silhouettes are prepared behind the entry overlay, so their first appearance no longer stalls the game.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ on a desktop at High, drive at night and switch into first person, then swap vehicles and watch the arrival. By day, look out over the open sea from a coast.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1905, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

In the first buggy to hovercraft swap after entering a world alone, High and Medium built no new arrival shaders on either graphics backend.

### Known issues

- An occasional long frame after a bot arrives at Low is being looked at.
- At dawn, fog lies on the sea along coasts.
- Other vehicles do not have headlight beams yet.
- On dry ground, a vehicle left on its roof still rights itself after a few seconds; whether it should is the owner's to decide.
- How the chat card and the keyboard behave on real phones is not yet confirmed.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
