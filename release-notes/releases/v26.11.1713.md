## Syn3h v26.11.1713

A fast Zorb now skips across water and lava like a stone, a new planet gives you a fresh vehicle, and bots stop coming and going when the frame rate wobbles.

Public release at 9:00pm Pacific on 17 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

A Zorb that rolled into water or lava at speed used to bog down at once. Now, above about 10 m/s on a shallow entry, it bounces off the surface and skims with very little friction, each skip a little shorter, like a stone thrown flat across a pond. Slower, it settles in and floats as before.

### Features

- A fast Zorb skips across water and lava like a stone. Above about 10 m/s on a shallow entry it bounces off the surface and skims with very little friction, each skip a little shorter. Slower, it settles in and floats as before, and lava is still a struggle once it settles in.
- A new host setting beside the bot count, "Keep this many bots whatever the frame rate", keeps every bot the host asked for even when frames are slow or automatic quality drops a level. Bots still arrive one at a time.
- Steering works while skipping, and Space hops the ball clear of the surface at speed; it lands and keeps skipping. A skip off a wave face goes a different way from one off flat water.

### Fixes

- Entering a different planet now gives you a fresh vehicle: Zorb off, wings closed, held inputs cleared and cords dropped. Returning to the same world from the lobby, a reconnect, and a host changing a world setting keep your modes; switching vehicle in place still keeps Zorb and wings; your selected gear, vehicle, colours and settings are kept.
- v26.11.1712 listed that the lobby might draw slower on a slow phone with the new touch controls. It was measured and does not happen: the lobby draws at the same rate with and without them; the earlier difference was noise from a busy test machine.
- Waves on a planet's water no longer carry over briefly from the previous planet after a planet change.
- A bot sent away to keep the frame rate up stays away for at least five minutes, instead of rejoining and leaving again each time the quality level changes. A quality level's larger bot allowance only takes effect once that level has held for a minute.

### Handling

- A Zorb entering water at 20 m/s now loses about 1.4 m/s in its first second, down from 9.4. At 30 m/s and a shallow angle it skips three to five times and keeps most of its speed.
- Speed on open water is now uncapped, as on flat land: rolling forward from rest for 20 s reaches about 110 m/s on water, 114 m/s on lava and 119 m/s on flat land. Raise Skip friction for a ceiling.
- Four new world settings under Zorb, so a room can tune skipping by feel:
  - Skip speed, 10 m/s: the speed at which skimming lift carries the ball.
  - Skip entry angle, 30°: the steepest entry that still skips; steeper entries dig in.
  - Skip friction, 0.03: drag along the surface while skimming (0.03 slows a skimming ball about 0.3 m/s each second).
  - Energy kept per skip, 0.5: how bouncy each skip is.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/, switch to the Zorb on a world with water, build up speed and roll onto the water at a shallow angle.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1713, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Not measured for this release.

### Known issues

- A Space press in mid-air between skips is remembered for only 0.25 seconds (the same as over land), so on a long skip a hop can be missed. Press it as the ball touches the surface.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
