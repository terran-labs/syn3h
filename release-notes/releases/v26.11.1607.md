## Syn3h v26.11.1607

Buggies that never stay boxes, vehicle switches that keep your speed and a clean address in the public room.

Public release at 3:00pm Pacific on 16 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

Some players saw their buggy as a plain box for a whole session: if its model failed to download once, it never tried again until the page was reloaded. That is fixed. Switching vehicles now carries on from where you were instead of starting over, tag beams start from the vehicle that sends them, and the public room no longer puts a room name in your address or your invite links. Sun shafts also come up to full strength by default.

### Features

- Switching vehicles keeps you where you are, facing the same way and moving as you were. A switch in mid-air stays in the air, and an upside-down vehicle comes out upright.
- The address stays clean in the public room, and invite links and QR codes no longer carry a room name. Old links with the public room's name still work and are tidied. Private rooms keep their name in the link, and links to a pinned version stay pinned.
- Sun shafts are stronger by default.
- The Updates tab now lists the version you are playing, including this one.

### Fixes

- If your buggy ever appeared as a plain box until you reloaded, that is fixed: a failed download now retries on its own.
- Tag beams now come from your vehicle, not from behind it. A Grapple cord seen on another player's vehicle also starts from where that vehicle is drawn.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ in a browser. Drag to look around the galaxy and tap a world to go there. Change vehicle from the Me card's Vehicle menu at any speed and carry on. Invite friends from Players & session; the link opens your world in the public room.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1607, whatever ships after it.
