## Syn3h v26.11.1803

Rivers that look like flowing water, and no glow on a hill that hides the sun.

Public release at 7:48pm Pacific on 18 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

Rivers were drawn as a pale white sheet laid over the land. Now they read as flowing water. Shallow water is clear and shows the bed, the banks fade in softly with no hard edge, and white water appears only where it should: at drops, steps and fast shallows, streaming along the flow. Waterfalls show ropes of falling water, even from a distance. And with a low sun behind a hill, the haze in front of the hill no longer glows as if the sun were shining through it.

### Features

No new features in this release.

### Fixes

- Rivers read as flowing water: shallow water is clear and shows the bed underneath.
- River banks fade in softly, with no hard edge.
- White water appears only at drops, steps and fast shallows, and streams along the flow.
- Waterfalls show ropes of water from a distance.
- With a low sun behind a hill, the hill's face no longer glows where the sun is hidden.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/, find a river, and follow it down to a waterfall.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1803, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Not measured for this release.

### Known issues

- Desert rivers stay a pale mint, from that world's palette.
- On phones at Low and Medium, lakes and rivers are drawn flatter than the waves vehicles ride, by up to about a quarter of a metre.
- How the chat card and the keyboard behave on real phones is not yet confirmed.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
