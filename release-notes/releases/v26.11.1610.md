## Syn3h v26.11.1610

A new galaxy in the lobby, water and lava you can drive through, and labels that stay put.

Public release at 9:02pm Pacific on 16 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

The lobby's planets used to be the same few spheres in different paints. Now each is painted from its own world, at its true share of land and sea, with its own air, clouds and night glow, and named in a label beside it. Driving into water used to sink the buggy and lava held it like glue; now it floats and paddles in water, rides and waddles through lava, steers while afloat, and every sea, lake and lava flow shows real waves that match what it floats on. Vehicle labels, which slid around and hid behind your own wings, now sit still just above each vehicle. Vehicles arrive and leave in a burst of light, the Zorb bounces, and the HUD stays readable over pale sky, ice and lava.

### Features

- Lobby planets: each is painted from its real terrain, at its true land and sea share, with its own air, clouds and night glow. Orbits are ordered by physical sense with small tilts, and the Moon circles Alpine. Labels show the world's name over its biome, sit beside their planet in its colour with no connector lines, and keep the biome line whenever it fits. The swamp is now Mire, and the Desert is dry.
- Minimap: each world's own colours, with water, lava and ice where they are. Your arrow and other people are shaded darts in their vehicle's paint, bots smaller triangles, and vehicles off the map are luminous ticks on a ring, longer and brighter when near.
- Water: the buggy floats half in, paddles at about 5 m/s, skims at speed and bobs on real waves. Opening the wings under water floats it up, steerable, instead of launching it into the air.
- Lava: the buggy rides, porpoises and waddles through at about 3 m/s; crossing a 30 m pool takes about 6 s, where before it never crossed.
- Steering afloat: in lava full lock turns the buggy at about 35 degrees a second, and in water about 54, so you can turn round and drive out.
- The look of liquids: the sea, lakes, rivers and lava show real waves that match what vehicles float on. From the air the sea is saturated water with visible swell and foam where waves break; under water is bright and coloured by the planet, with caustics and light shafts. Lava has a crust, glowing cracks, bubbles, flames and embers, and a glowing shoreline. Splashes are sized by the impact, white in sunlight and molten in lava, and a Zorb skipping across water splashes too.
- Ice: the Ice Moon's sea is an ice sheet with floes and cracks that you can drive on. Tyres lose grip on ice (braking from 25 m/s takes 41 m against 17 m on dry ground) and a little on a wet shore.
- Vehicle labels: no connector lines; each sits just above its vehicle and fades in and out instead of sliding. Your own wings, body, Zorb or skirt never hide one. People are named out to 1,500 m and bots to 300 m, shrinking to a small name with distance, on lighter glass.
- Arrivals: a vehicle arrives in a cloud streaming into a hot core, a beam, a ground light that becomes a shock ring, and resolves in light in its own colours; it is visible from a distance by day and lighter on phones. Leaving plays it in reverse.
- The Zorb bounces: a first rebound of about 30 percent of the drop, settling in a few bounces. Holding brake inside it deadens the bounce so a landing sticks; Space pops the ball about 4.5 m up with a tumble. Bounciness now runs 0 to 2 and Hop height 0 to 12 m; saved worlds keep their old behaviour exactly.
- HUD text and the focus display stay readable over pale sky, ice and lava, with a soft dark edge and no boxes.

### Fixes

- The buggy could barely steer while afloat in lava or water: the floating hull never saw the steering. It steers now.
- The buggy's wings no longer vanish over water at some angles.
- The right-hand sidebar no longer blocks every vehicle label on that side of the screen.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ in a browser. Drag to look around the galaxy and tap a world to go there. Drive into a lake or a lava flow and steer your way out; try the Ice Moon's frozen sea; in a Zorb, hold brake to stick a landing and press Space to hop.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1610, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Not measured for this release. On the builder's runs the liquid effects cost about 0.2 ms more a frame on a desktop with a big splash on screen and about 1.8 ms on a slowed phone stand-in; lava's heavier effects are off below Medium.

### Known issues

- Deserts saved before this release keep their old shape; old Desert links open the dry Desert.
- Ice Moons saved before the frozen sea keep open water.
- A gamepad button held down while the About guide is open takes effect when the guide closes.
- Touch aiming was tested with simulated touches in a phone-sized browser, not yet on a physical phone.
