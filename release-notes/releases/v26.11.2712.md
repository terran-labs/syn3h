## Mira v26.11.2712

### Features

Liquid uses the Search shader for sidebar cards when their stack fits.

### Fixes

The five cards previously picked up a separate gray fill and bright rim even when the sidebar had no room to scroll.

### Handling

This release changes the driving interface.

### Play

Adjust Tint in Settings to change Search and the five cards together.

### Performance

The existing glass pass draws the visible cards. When the whole stack can scroll, the browser paints the cards with their content.

### Known issues

The scrolling fallback approximates the shader.
