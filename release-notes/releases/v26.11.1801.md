## Syn3h v26.11.1801

No sun rays or lens flare under water or lava, and no rays left hanging in the sky after sunset.

Public release at 3:30pm Pacific on 18 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

Sun rays and the lens flare kept drawing when the camera went under water or into lava, where no sunlight should reach. They now fade out as the camera passes through the surface, smoothly, with no pop. And after sunset, rays could stay painted across the night sky from the last daylight; they now go with the sun.

### Features

No new features in this release.

### Fixes

- No sun rays, sky rays or lens flare with the camera under water or lava. They fade smoothly as the camera crosses the surface, and come back the same way as it rises.
- Sun rays no longer linger in the sky after sunset.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/, drive into a lake or a lava field with the sun low, and watch the rays fade as the camera goes under.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1801, whatever ships after it.
