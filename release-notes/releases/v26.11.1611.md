## Syn3h v26.11.1611

The Syn3h name in the galaxy, sunrise rays, chat, and planets of any size.

Public release at 11:00pm Pacific on 16 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

The lobby opened on a flat logo over the galaxy, and the loading view repeated it. Now the Syn3h name stands in the lobby's own space along an orbit, with a push-in opening on the first load, and the loading view shows one playful line instead. Each planet's day now turns at its own pace, set by its distance from the sun and the same for everyone in a room, and a low sun throws rays that ridges, clouds and vehicles cut. Players can talk: chat opens with Y, and bots join in, answer requests and tow each other out of trouble. Planets can be any size from 1,024 to 4,096 m, grow extra mountains and islands, and the Archipelago is a real archipelago. Labels hold one steady glass, tyre tracks are back on soft ground, and a bot or player who leaves is gone at once.

### Features

- Lobby: the Syn3h name sits in the scene along an orbit, with a push-in opening on first load (about 9 s, about 3 s on later loads in a session, the name alone replaying when you return from a world). It hangs below the planets when the camera goes under the plane, and clicking a world un-reveals it. Each planet glows in its own colour when pointed at. The layout chooser is gone, and the update banner sits at the top centre.
- Loading: one playful line and no logo, with the seconds and the real step after ten seconds.
- Sunrise and sunset throw visible rays from the sun, fixed in the world as the camera turns and the same for every player in a room. Ridges, cloud banks and vehicles cut gaps into them. Nothing at noon.
- Day and night: each planet's day turns by its orbit and every player in a room sees the same hour. Days run from 2 min 45 s on Volcanic, nearest the sun, to 9 min on the Ice Moon, about five minutes mid-system; the Moon shares Alpine's day. A Day length slider overrides it. Sunrise no longer washes out or floods green. The music's night theme follows the hour.
- Chat: Y opens it, from a pill beside the minimap on desktop or a round button on phones, with an unread count. Lines are cleaned, filtered and rate limited by the room and never stored. Tap a name to mute it; the host can turn chat off.
- Bots chat in eight voices about what is happening and answer requests such as "follow me", "stop", "race me" and "quiet", following for a while and peeling off with a line. They notice a stuck bot and tow it out with the grapple, arguing about who goes, and one accident-prone bot has the odd mishap. Settings cover chat, bot chatter and its level, bots rescuing each other, and mishaps per hour.
- Planet size: any size from 1,024 to 4,096 m in 256 m steps, with the true radius shown. Saved worlds and links keep their old sizes.
- Landforms: extra peaks on each planet by biome and optional extra islands. The Archipelago is now nine islands on a 2,048 m tile.
- A fresh visit lands on a random planet seed; reloads and links keep theirs.
- The jet's lift and drag and the buggy's wings follow the planet's air: on the airless Moon the jet flies on thrust alone.
- World settings in real units say so in their labels: gravity in m/s², heights in metres, wind in m/s.
- Vehicle labels: one consistent glass everywhere, with no darker plate over the speed or height readout. A label eases in and out, dims to half behind terrain, grows upward when it gains a line, and shows a quiet "+N" when it hides others behind it.
- Tyre tracks are visible again on soil, sand and grass in plain driving, stronger in slides and braking, and last about 500 m at speed. Ice keeps a pale scuff.

### Fixes

- A bot that is removed, or a player who leaves, is gone from the player list, minimap and labels at once instead of about five seconds later. Only the leaving cloud plays out.
- Arrivals now finish on time on a slow device: a one-second hold no longer stretches to two at 5 frames a second.
- The lobby no longer falls back to the flat logo because a one-off start-up step ran slow, and devices without half-float render targets now get the name in the scene. The flat mark remains only when the device cannot build the shader or loses its graphics context, and it matches the new look.
- Phone landscape: the lobby's labels are no longer crammed to the left on first load.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ in a browser. Watch the name arrive, then tap a world. Press Y to say hello; ask a bot to "follow me" or "race me". Wait for a sunrise and drive into the rays. In World settings, try a 4,096 m planet or the new Archipelago.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1611, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Not measured for this release. In the lobby check, pointing at a planet to make it glow left the frame time unchanged (2.9 ms against 3.0 ms).

### Known issues

- Planets between the three old sizes look wrong to players on frozen older versions.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
- Deserts saved before v26.11.1610 keep their old shape; old Desert links open the dry Desert.
- Ice Moons saved before the frozen sea keep open water.
- Touch aiming was tested with simulated touches in a phone-sized browser, not yet on a physical phone.
