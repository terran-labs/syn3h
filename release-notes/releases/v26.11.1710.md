## Syn3h v26.11.1710

A tap to begin where sound is blocked, softer tyre tracks, gentle sun rays at every quality level, and bigger names on near planets.

Public release at 6:00pm Pacific on 17 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

On iPhones and for first-time visitors, the browser often holds sound back until the first tap, so the opening played in silence. Where that happens the game now waits on the opening's still first frame with a short line until you tap, and the Big Bang and its music start together. Tyre tracks are now a gentle shade of the ground that grows from the wheels, and motor sparks only fly when the motor is working hard. Sun rays in the world now show at any sun height and at every quality level, much softer than before. In the lobby, the names of nearby planets are larger.

### Features

- Where the browser blocks sound, the opening waits for one tap, click or key, then the Big Bang and the music start together. Where sound is allowed nothing changes, and invite links and direct links into a world never wait.
- Sun rays now show at any sun height and at every quality level, much softer than before. New settings for their strength, high-sun share, reach and length; the default strength is lower than before (0.55 against 1).
- Lobby planet names grow with their planet on screen, up to 1.9 times their size for the nearest planets. A new Graphics setting, Lobby caption scale, sets the largest size.
- New settings: Tyre mark fade-in (Graphics), plus Motor sparks from load and Motor spark amount (Buggy).

### Fixes

- Tyre tracks are softer: a gentle shade of the ground under them that holds up on every world and at any time of day, faint when you cruise and stronger when you skid, spin or brake hard.
- New track grows smoothly from the wheels instead of flashing in behind them.
- Motor sparks fly only when the motor is at full stretch, like launching from a standstill or crawling up a steep climb, not while you cruise, coast, brake or fly.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ on an iPhone and tap to begin; then drive the buggy hard across a world in low sun and watch the tracks and the rays.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1710, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Sun rays now draw at Minimal and Low, which drew none before; the builder measured 0.1 to 0.3 ms of extra GPU time per frame there.

### Known issues

- These design changes are shipped for the owner to adjust on the live game.
- Near a screen edge a large planet name can be cut off.
- On a phone held sideways, the jet's throttle-down button overlaps the navigation buttons. The fix is on the touch controls branch.
- On an iPad, the navigation buttons overlap the King of the Hill winner announcement.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
