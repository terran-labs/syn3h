## Syn3h v26.11.1612

Quiet planet names in the lobby, and no hitch on arrivals.

Public release at 11:30pm Pacific on 16 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

The lobby's planet labels sat on glass plates that pulled the eye away from the galaxy. Now each planet's name is a quiet caption: plain small capitals under the planet, its biome beneath, fixed to it and hidden when another planet or the sun passes in front. At High quality on desktop, every arrival, vehicle switch or departure used to stall the game for a quarter of a second while the scene rebuilt its lighting; that is gone.

### Features

- Lobby: planet names are quiet captions in plain small capitals under each planet, with the biome beneath, no plate or border. They stay fixed to their planet and are hidden by any planet or the sun in front of them.

### Fixes

- At High quality and above on desktop, a vehicle arriving, switching or leaving no longer causes a quarter-second hitch. Measured on a desktop at High: the worst frame during an arrival fell from 250 to 475 ms to under 30 ms.
- Leaving the lobby for a world no longer cuts off the lobby's check of how many people are on each planet.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ in a browser. Drag the galaxy round and watch the planet names slip behind each other, then tap a world.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1612, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Measured for the arrival fix only, on a desktop at High with the browser's own frame timing: an arrival rebuilt 251 materials (413 to 749 ms of building) before, and 6 (about 7 ms) after. Medium, Low and touch devices never had the cost.

### Known issues

- Planets between the three old sizes look wrong to players on frozen older versions.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
- Touch aiming was tested with simulated touches in a phone-sized browser, not yet on a physical phone.
