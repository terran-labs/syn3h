## Syn3h v26.11.1604

Aim by touch, a left-handed layout, the About guide in play and a tidier phone screen.

Public release at 11:40am Pacific on 16 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

On a phone, the only way to look around used to be the vehicle itself. Now a drag on the open game view turns the camera, and a left-handed setting mirrors the whole touch layout. The About guide, until now only in the lobby, opens over the running world without leaving it. The phone screen got tidier too: the minimap no longer sits on your vehicle, and messages fit on one line clear of the controls. Name labels above vehicles were ready in time but failed a two-player check, so they wait for the next release.

### Features

- Touch aiming: drag anywhere on the open game view, opposite the stick, to turn the camera. A touch that starts on a button or the minimap never aims.
- Left-handed setting: on the Me card, it mirrors the stick, the action buttons, the hint and the minimap. It is kept on this device.
- About in play: open "About & how to play" from Players & session, or from the Paused screen. It opens over the world; the room, the address and pause stay as they were, and Escape or Back closes it. Opening it sets down any gear in hand without using it.
- On phones the minimap docks in the top right corner instead of covering your vehicle, and in landscape the speed and height readouts move up out of the stick's reach.

### Fixes

- Messages on phones fit on one line and stay clear of the readouts and controls. On desktop they sit below the heading line.
- Joining the public room now says "Joined as" and your name; a long room name is shortened in the middle.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ in a browser. Drag to look around the galaxy and tap a world to go there. On a phone, drive with the stick and drag on the open view to aim; switch hands from the Me card in the sidebar. Open the About guide from Players & session or the Paused screen.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1604, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Not measured for this release.

### Known issues

- A gamepad button held down while the About guide is open takes effect when the guide closes.
- Touch aiming was tested with simulated touches in a phone-sized browser, not yet on a physical phone.
