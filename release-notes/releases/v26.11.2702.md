## Mira v26.11.2702

### Features

The Panels button shows the unread chat count on touch screens.

### Fixes

- On sideways phones, driving notices share the bottom edge with the minimap and thumb controls. Room notices take priority over offers and the race readout.
- The Daydreaming line fits between the controls and yields to driving notices.
- Pause uses the same dimming and blur as Leave, including its low-quality fallback.
- The altitude instrument reads ALT ASL for altitude above sea level. The diagnostics counter gives the room toast space.
- The reset button has room inside the card's rounded lower corners.
- Enter can skip the opening after loading finishes, even before the first animation frame. Planet names can use a diagonal position beside a crowded lobby dock.

### Handling

Vehicle handling is unchanged.

### Play

Open Panels to read chat. On touch screens, its unread count clears when you read the messages.

### Performance

Frame cost was not measured for this interface update.

### Known issues

The iPhone graphics issues tracked separately remain open.
