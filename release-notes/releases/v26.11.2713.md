## Mira v26.11.2713

### Features

Nearby vehicles illuminate the contours of a steep hillside when their headlights are on.

### Fixes

Nearby headlights could leave triangular gaps on steep slopes. The light now follows the terrain grid and fades smoothly across its edges.

### Handling

This fix changes how the renderer lights the ground beneath a beam.

### Play

Turn on Headlights in Settings to see the beam after dusk. Your device remembers the setting and brightness.

### Performance

With one nearby hovercraft lighting the ridge, vehicle effects averaged 0.12 to 0.15 ms per update in two desktop samples. The previous beta measured 0.09 to 0.20 ms in the same scene.

### Known issues

The preferred reach of the buggy’s beam is still open for feedback from the chase camera.
