## Mira v26.11.2705

### Features

Quality offers show a check beside Keep and a directional arrow beside the proposed level. The controls sit together in the centre of the offer.

### Fixes

On a phone, closing Chat could flash the panel at full opacity after it had faded out. The sidebar now holds the transparent frame until it is hidden.

### Handling

This update changes interface controls.

### Play

Press Escape in the chat field to return to driving. In Settings, the level buttons align with the quality selector.

### Performance

The closing animation uses its existing duration.

### Known issues

The Chat flash was reproduced at phone size. The same check at desktop size already ended at zero opacity.
