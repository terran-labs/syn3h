## Syn3h v26.11.1703

Clean sunsets behind the hills, and J closes the invite.

Public release at 6:00am Pacific on 17 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

Two small fixes. When the sun sat low just behind a ridge, the sunrise rays could paint cyan and dark red blotches along the hilltop; the rays now dim the land behind them without changing its colour. And J, which opens the invite link and QR code in play, now closes it again even after you have clicked Copy.

### Fixes

- A low sun behind a ridge no longer paints coloured blotches along the hilltop. The sun's rays and burst are unchanged.
- J closes the invite view from any control inside it, including the Copy button just after you click it. Space and Enter on a focused button still press it.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ in a browser, drive in the Badlands towards a hill as the sun sets behind it, and press J to share the room.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1703, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Not measured for this release.

### Known issues

- Players on an older build in the same room still see the old long names for the same bots.
- Planets between the three old sizes look wrong to players on frozen older versions.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
- Touch aiming was tested with simulated touches in a phone-sized browser, not yet on a physical phone.
