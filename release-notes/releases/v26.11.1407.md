### The story so far

Work turned toward the demo, set for Sunday noon at a game jam, where people would join from their phones while a projector showed the game. Invite links got QR codes drawn without any library and a projector view for joining, and an attract mode runs idle shots through a cinematic camera when nobody is playing. Test browsers had been reporting slow frames; a profile in a real Chrome on the build machine showed about 8 ms a frame, and the slow readings came from the headless test browsers. A promotion script and a preview service for a frozen stable build were added, the first step toward a channel that does not change under the audience.

### Features

- Invite QR codes and a projector join view
- Attract mode: idle shots through a cinematic camera
- The hovercraft glides across terrain and liquid
- Beams for the tag game in the Syn3h green, with effects on other players' vehicles too
- Scores and a coloured countdown at the end of a round
- A promotion script and server for a frozen stable build
- The hill's walls fade near the camera, with posts on the ground marking the zone, and moving bots contest the hill
