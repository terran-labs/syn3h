### The story so far

The open test landscape became a volcanic island, on the owner's direction that every world should be generated from a shared seed with the volcano at its centre. The generator now builds one island per seed: the volcano at the origin with a crown of rock round its crater and a lava lake below the summit, eight spawn pads round the coast facing inward, a road that climbs from the first pad to the summit ring, and a ring road round its foot, all inside a lava sea. Lava was drawn in the game for the first time, with a glowing shoreline, rivers, pools and flame vents. A 24 case driving sweep recorded how each vehicle handled, and buggies that were getting stuck on their roofs got corrected crash friction and a timed rescue that sets them upright in place.

### Features

- One island per seed, with the volcano at the centre, eight spawn pads and roads to the summit
- Lava drawn in the game: the sea out to the horizon, the crater lake, rivers, pools and flame vents
- Match announcements and hill ownership on screen
- Share artwork, app icons and page metadata

### Fixes

- Buggies stuck on their roof or side are set upright after three seconds, keeping their heading
- Crash friction is corrected, so buggies no longer trip into rolls as easily
