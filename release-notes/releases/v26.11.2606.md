## Mira v26.11.2606

Smoother play: the control tips no longer cause a hitch.

Public release at 4:48pm Pacific on 26 November 2026, live at https://mira.cc/.

### The story so far

A beta on top of v26.11.2605. A profile of play found a hitch four times a second, which is gone, and the owner's test of v26.11.2605 brought a handful of interface changes: the control tips box is now off by default, Leave this world is a solid dialog, sideways phones get a full-height panels list, and the lobby's links are a compact pair.

### Features

No new features in this release.

### Fixes

- Smoother play: removed a hitch that happened four times a second.
- The control tips box is off by default; H still shows all controls. It can be switched on in Settings, Controls.
- Leave this world: a solid dialog over a dimmed, blurred screen.
- Phones held sideways: the panels list reaches the bottom of the screen, and Close panels is readable over bright skies.
- Lobby: the version and site links sit closer together at the lower left.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://mira.cc/ and play as usual.

This exact version stays playable at https://mira.cc/?v=26.11.2606, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

The new sea's cost, measured with GPU timestamps on WebGL 2 against v26.11.2500: unchanged at Minimal and Low, about 0.5 ms more at Medium, 1.4 to 1.9 ms more at High, and more again at Ultra and Cinematic. It builds nothing in play.

- The cause of the black flashes with sun shadows on is not yet fixed, only avoided by turning the shadows off.
- On the same devices water refraction, the force field contact glow and the headlight beams' soft edge are still off.
- The graphics process can still stop on iPhone.

- After a climb to Ultra or Cinematic, a bot vehicle's mid-distance model can occasionally build once during play.

- Not yet tested in Safari with WebGPU.
- Which side of a sideways iPhone has the camera cutout is worked out from the screen's rotation and has not been checked on a real iPhone.
