## Mira v26.11.2304

Test links for tracking the black flashes on iPhone.

Public release at 3:11pm Pacific on 23 November 2026, live at https://mira.cc/.

### The story so far

The black flashes on iPhone show at Medium and High on WebGPU, and not at Low. To find which difference between those levels causes them, this release adds links that switch one thing back at a time, and a counter that shows blank frames as they happen. Without them in the address, nothing changes.

### Features

- Test links for tracking the black flashes on iPhone: ?blank=1 shows a counter of blank frames with a Test flash button; ?shadows=0, ?post=base, ?samples=1, ?shading=0, ?lowrest=1 and ?freeze=1 each switch one thing off for the visit. They change nothing unless added to the address.

### Fixes

No other fixes in this release.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://mira.cc/?gfx=2&blank=1 on an iPhone, enter a world, and watch the counter.

This exact version stays playable at https://mira.cc/?v=26.11.2304, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

No performance changes in this release; the links change nothing unless added to the address.
