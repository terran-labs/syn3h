### The story so far

Five minutes after the buggy, the lobby followed. It has sliding panels, a menu and title music, and it remembers your vehicle and colours with a 3D preview. The jet and hovercraft became selectable, camera modes arrived with travel smoothing and terrain clearance, and multiplayer learned player identities, name tags, room names in the address and bots that everyone in a room can see.

### Features

- The lobby with sliding panels, a menu, title music and a settings panel
- Saved vehicle and colour choices with a 3D preview
- The jet and hovercraft as selectable vehicles, with the hovercraft model
- Camera modes, with smoothing and terrain clearance
- Name tags, room names in the address, and bots owned by the host and seen by everyone
