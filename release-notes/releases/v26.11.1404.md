### The story so far

Five minutes after the buggy, the lobby followed. It was rebuilt from the recovered Syn3h interface, with sliding panels, a menu and title music, and it remembers your vehicle and colours with a 3D preview. Where pieces of the old interface could not be found, the gaps were written down instead of guessed. The jet and hovercraft became selectable, the Syn3h camera modes were ported with travel smoothing and terrain clearance, and multiplayer learned player identities, name tags, room names in the address and bots that everyone in a room can see.

### Features

- The Syn3h lobby with sliding panels, a menu, title music and a settings panel
- Saved vehicle and colour choices with a 3D preview
- The jet and hovercraft as selectable vehicles, with the recovered hovercraft model
- The Syn3h camera modes, with smoothing and terrain clearance
- Name tags, room names in the address, and bots owned by the host and seen by everyone
