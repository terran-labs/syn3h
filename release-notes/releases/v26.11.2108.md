## Syn3h v26.11.2108

Fewer quality-drop hitches with bots or other players in the room.

Public release at 4:45am Pacific on 21 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

The last few releases stopped quality drops hitching in an empty room, but with bots or other players around, each automatic drop still built shaders for their vehicles and could stall for up to a second. Now those vehicles are prepared for the lower levels too, so with bots or other players in the room, automatic quality drops between High, Medium and Low no longer hitch, when you enter at High or below. The step to the lowest level can still build a few shaders.

### Features

No new features in this release.

### Fixes

- With bots or other players in the room, automatic quality drops between High, Medium and Low no longer hitch, when you enter at High or below.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ at High, start a room with bots, and play until the game steps its quality down.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.2108, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

In our check on the release build with four bots in the room, the steps from High to Medium and from Medium to Low built no shaders at desktop and phone size; the step to the lowest level built 5 graphs and 3 pipelines. Loading is about 10 ms longer on either graphics path.

### Known issues

- With others in the room, the step to the lowest quality level can still build a few shaders (5 graphs and 3 pipelines in our check); under investigation.
- Another player's jet or hovercraft can cause a small hitch when it first appears.
- Entering a world at Ultra or Cinematic, the first automatic drop can still hitch.
- The in-game performance check that found this has not yet been run for the jet and the buggy at their own detail-switch distances.
- No phone has been measured for these quality steps.
- On the older graphics path (WebGL 2), changing vehicle at Low still builds a few shaders.
- The experimental glass shader has not been timed on any phone. Merging buttons and the press effect are pilots on one pair of buttons.
- The sun rays' GPU cost was not measured, and whether they flicker while driving was judged by eye only.
- Changing the quality level in play rebuilds the firework batches.
- A player still on the previous version sees Plasma Thunder as it was and ignores the new messages. Their version never reports where its bolts end, so players on this version burst those bolts on their own after 1.5 s, and those bursts push nobody.
- The first touch on a phone builds one effect pipeline.
- On WebGL 2, preparation spends about 3 s waiting between completion checks.
- Not measured on a physical iPhone; touch was emulated on a Mac.
- The button glass is drawn with CSS; the experimental glass shader is off by default.
- Phone behaviour was tested in browser engines on a Mac, not on a physical iPhone.
- A player on an older version whose cord holds someone cannot be unhooked by the new stop words; stretching the cord still frees them.
- At dawn, fog lies on the sea along coasts.
- Bots' vehicles do not show headlight beams in mist yet.
- On dry ground, a vehicle left on its roof still rights itself after a few seconds; whether it should is the owner's to decide.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
