## Syn3h v26.11.2201

Quality changes no longer stall play, splashes for Tag and Thunder on water, and rings that hold against a bright sky.

Public release at 9:00pm Pacific on 22 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

When the game changes its quality level, by itself or because you picked one, it now prepares everything the new level needs before play carries on: post effects, the waterline, ground cover, spray and foam, particles, vehicle shadows, bots' distant looks and every gear's effects. Before, some of that was built in the middle of play, which showed as a short stall. Tag and Thunder strikes that land on water or lava now throw a splash. And the rings from Tag, Thunder and Yoink, and the Chaser, carry a thin dark edge by day so they hold against a bright sky.

### Features

- Tag and Thunder strikes on water or lava throw the same splash as a vehicle landing in it.
- The rings from Tag, Thunder and Yoink, and the Chaser, have a thin dark edge by day; at night only inside your own headlight beam.
- The performance check has rows for the Chaser and Yoink.

### Fixes

- After a quality change, automatic or by hand, the game prepares the new level's looks before play resumes.
- A quality level picked by hand no longer stalls when bots arrive and post effects ease off.
- Thunder on your own car: the stars near the lens are narrower and shorter too.
- Ring edges stay a sensible width on screen, near or far.
- On a phone, the Running slow notice no longer blocks steering: a drag that starts on it steers, and a tap still presses its buttons.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/, pick an Archipelago world with four bots, and use Tag and Thunder over the sea.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.2201, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

In our checks with four bots, a climb from Minimal to Low built nothing in play on either graphics path, including a dive into deep water afterwards, and Medium to High built nothing on WebGPU. Low to Medium is unproven on WebGPU. The performance check reads 0/0 in every gear row, including the new Chaser and Yoink rows.

### Known issues

- After the quality climbs from Low to Medium, a bot's shadow can build once.
- After a quality climb, a bot's jet can build its looks once in play, mostly after entering at the lowest level. A fix is being checked.
- At Tint settings below a style's default, text can be pale over bright scenes; that is the player's choice.
- The Glass style is removed; a saved Glass choice becomes Frost.
- For the first instant, the burst's streaks still cross most of the screen.
- A player still on a version before v26.11.2118 who pauses is treated as hidden, so the host role passes after 3 seconds instead of 20.
- Liquid has not been timed on any phone.
- Entering a world at Ultra or Cinematic, the first automatic drop can still hitch.
- On the older graphics path, choosing a top quality level before pressing Play makes entering a world slower.
- On the older graphics path, changing vehicle at Low can cause a small hitch.
- The in-game performance check that found this has not yet been run for the jet and the buggy at their own detail-switch distances.
- No phone has been measured for these quality steps.
- The sun rays' GPU cost was not measured, and whether they flicker while driving was judged by eye only.
- Changing the quality level in play rebuilds the firework batches.
- A player still on the previous version sees Plasma Thunder as it was and ignores the new messages. Their version never reports where its bolts end, so players on this version burst those bolts on their own after 1.5 s, and those bursts push nobody.
- The first touch on a phone builds one effect pipeline.
- On WebGL 2, preparation spends about 3 s waiting between completion checks.
- Not measured on a physical iPhone; touch was emulated on a Mac.
- Phone behaviour was tested in browser engines on a Mac, not on a physical iPhone.
- A player on an older version whose cord holds someone cannot be unhooked by the new stop words; stretching the cord still frees them.
- At dawn, fog lies on the sea along coasts.
- On dry ground, a vehicle left on its roof still rights itself after a few seconds; whether it should is the owner's to decide.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
