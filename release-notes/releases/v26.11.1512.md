## Syn3h v26.11.1512

Lobby buttons open their panels directly, with clearer spacing below.

Public release at 3:44pm Pacific on 15 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

The galaxy stays visible while idle. Multiplayer, Settings and About now open their panels directly, removing the intermediate welcome screen and its one-second exit animation.

### Features

- Open a lobby panel in a 240 ms transition. Switch panels directly, or close with Escape, Back or a tap outside.

### Fixes

- Separated the build footer from the persistent lobby buttons so they do not overlap when a panel is open.
- Softened the galaxy backdrop around the controls and preserved reduced-motion behaviour.

### Play

[Play this exact release](https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1512). The plain address serves the latest accepted release.
