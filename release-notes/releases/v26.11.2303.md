## Mira v26.11.2303

A recorder for black flashes.

Public release at 9:00pm Pacific on 23 November 2026, live at https://mira.cc/.

### The story so far

To help track the black flashes reported on iPhone, Diagnostics gains a switch that counts frames that go black and notes what happened in each one. It is off by default, and the copied report now always says which graphics path is in use and whether the graphics card reported any errors.

### Features

- "Record blank frames" switch in Performance, Diagnostics: counts frames that go black and logs what happened in them, for tracking the black flashes reported on iPhone. Off by default; costs about 5 ms a frame while on.

### Fixes

No other fixes in this release.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://mira.cc/, enter a world, open Performance, then Diagnostics, and turn on Record blank frames.

This exact version stays playable at https://mira.cc/?v=26.11.2303, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

While Record blank frames is on, it costs about 5 ms a frame; off, nothing measurable.
