## Syn3h v26.11.1900

The game never moves you for where you drove, plus a tidier HUD, watery wakes and grass that swishes flat.

Public release at 1:00am Pacific on 19 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

The game never moves you for where you drove. Lava and water are places to explore: nothing resets, respawns or rights you there. A buggy on its roof in lava or water floats back onto its wheels, a jet can climb out of lava under its own lift, and King of the Hill no longer sends you to your pad from the lava. When you are on your side or roof and nearly still, the game offers Flip upright.

The rest are design changes, shipped for the owner to adjust on the live game. Every part of the HUD now sits the same distance from the screen edge, and the gear switcher stays one size. On the water, vehicles used to leave stiff strips and rails of foam beside their hulls; their wakes now look like water, brightest where the vehicle touches the surface and breaking into lace that drifts apart and fades. On land, grass and flowers lay over where a buggy or a Zorb passes and rise back slowly.

### Features

- Flip upright (U, or FLIP on touch), offered when you are on your side or roof and nearly still.
- New settings, Wake strength and Wake width, to tune how much foam vehicles leave on the water.
- New settings, "How far wheels lay the grass over" and "Grass rises back after wheels, seconds", for how far plants bend and how long they take to rise.

### Fixes

- The game never moves you for where you drove. Lava and water are places to explore: nothing resets, respawns or rights you there.
- A buggy on its roof in lava or water floats back onto its wheels.
- A jet can climb out of lava under its own lift.
- King of the Hill no longer sends you to your pad from the lava.
- Every HUD element now sits the same distance from the screen edge.
- Wings, Zorb and Chat show their key beside the name.
- The gear switcher shows one gear either side of the selected one and stays the same size.
- A gear's explanation sits beside it, and name labels no longer cover it.
- Wakes look like water: foam is brightest behind what touches the water and breaks into lace that drifts apart and fades, never strips or rails.
- A Zorb leaves one trail, and a broken ring of foam at each skip.
- A buggy or a low jet in the water leaves one wide wake of breaking foam; a hovercraft leaves a ring of spray and a wide trail.
- Splashes keep their motion and read as water, with mist at the top and soft patches where the drops land.
- Grass and flowers are swished flat where a buggy or a Zorb passes, and rise back over about four seconds. They no longer wiggle under wheels. Under a hovercraft they still flutter in its wash.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/, drive into a lava pool and back out, then drive a Zorb or a hovercraft across water and through grass.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1900, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Not measured for this release.

### Known issues

- On dry ground, a vehicle left on its roof still rights itself after a few seconds; whether it should is the owner's to decide.
- These design changes are shipped for the owner to adjust on the live game.
- Desert rivers stay a pale mint, from that world's palette.
- On phones at Low and Medium, lakes and rivers are drawn flatter than the waves vehicles ride, by up to about a quarter of a metre.
- How the chat card and the keyboard behave on real phones is not yet confirmed.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
