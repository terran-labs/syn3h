## Syn3h v26.11.1514

Quality controls respond sooner to sustained slow rendering, vehicle switches preserve camera update order, and the Jet gains red tail lights.

Public release at 4:31pm Pacific on 15 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

This release follows the restored vehicle-switch and gear update. It adds quality relief while preserving manual choices, corrects camera update order after a switch, and adds the reviewed Jet tail lights.

### Features

- The quality registry defaults to High. Automatic quality reduces an unpinned level sooner after sustained pressure, while explicit player choices remain pinned.
- A named lower-quality recommendation appears during play when rendering stays slow after cheaper reductions are exhausted. Keep dismisses it for the session; accepting it selects the offered level.
- The Performance card shows the active level, automatic mode, pin state, reductions, pixel ratio and post-effects budget.
- Generated red strips sit in the Jet's outer nozzle housings, with adjustable brightness.

### Fixes

- An in-place vehicle switch inserts the new vehicle before the camera in the update list. This prevents the camera from reading the previous rendered pose after a switch.

### Play

Exact-build, multiplayer and frozen-version checks pass on both public addresses. All four vehicles pass in Chrome and WebKit, eight cases with no page errors.

### Known issues

- Earlier committed-code traces measured switched-vehicle pose lag falling from about 1.9 m to zero. They did not establish the requested 60 fps result. The earlier screen-step comparison also changed cadence and anti-aliasing, so it is not proof of an order-only visual improvement.
- The final bounded comparison stopped before browser launch when its source verification rejected a temporary path. The coordinator accepted the camera change under an explicit waiver using earlier evidence and regression tests. This release does not claim to fix all judder.
- The competitive bot-flee test retains its exact accepted-baseline failure. No test bounds were relaxed.
- The Jet half-throttle default, throttle gauge and new bot formation are not included.
