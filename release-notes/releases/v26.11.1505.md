## Syn3h v26.11.1505

Faster repeat vehicle swaps, generated thrust and clearer controls.

Public release at 1:02pm Pacific on 15 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

This release reduces repeat vehicle-switch stalls and fixes several visible problems from the noon build. The earlier judging build remains available through its frozen version link. Typecheck and 837 tests passed; one known tank-import check still fails. Both public addresses passed multiplayer and frozen-version checks, and all four vehicles passed in Chrome and WebKit.

### Features

- The jet has generated thrust plumes at its rear nozzles and belly ports, with length, brightness and colour controls.
- Two generated fans turn inside the hovercraft's rear duct. They stop in Zorb and resume on exit.

### Fixes

- Switching back to a vehicle reuses its existing model and compiled graphics programs. In the lane's browser run, repeat switches stayed below 170 ms; the first cold tank switch still paused for about three seconds. These measurements came from headless Chrome on a Mac.
- Vehicle switches retain heading and reset speed. Inactive cached vehicles stop stepping and colliding.
- The jet no longer draws floating lamp dots. Its gear retracts and its generated thrust disappears inside Zorb.
- The hovercraft rests its steering vanes and extinguishes its drive glow inside Zorb.
- Each vehicle's engine audio fades out over 0.2 seconds in Zorb and returns on exit. Vehicle event cues stay quiet inside the ball.
- The unsupported Explore choice is hidden. Tag game and King of the Hill remain available.
- Vehicle labels, panel headers and the phone gear selector are clearer; the gear selector stays clear of the speed readout and minimap.
- World arrival follows the player's current spawn after world regeneration. Returning from a cinematic camera shot eases back into the chase view.

### Play

[Play this exact release](https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1505). The plain address serves the latest accepted release.

- Choose another vehicle from the in-play selector.
- Press X for Zorb. Use E and Q to add or reduce jet lift or thrust.

### Known issues

- The first use of a vehicle can still pause while graphics programs compile, especially the tank.
- Buggy handling improvements were removed after the combined gate found two new camera-bounce failures. The existing hard-turn handling issue remains.
- A known test still flags tank design-study imports in the game dependency graph.
- The full materialization hold and its network protection are not part of this release.
- Grounded wing-open buggy audio can still contain a motor pulse; its separate correction is pending.
- The shallow underwater sky can still look too bright. Explore, underwater cloud attenuation and further lobby changes remain pending.
- Frozen versions share the live room server. Invitations created in a frozen version can open the latest build.
