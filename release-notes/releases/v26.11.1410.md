### The story so far

With the demo in view, decisions came quickly. At 4:45pm the frozen demo build was named the production release, to be served from Cloudflare so the audience could join from their phones, and at 5:00pm it was decided that it would replace the site then at syn3h.com that same night. Release and rollback scripts, a latency proxy and a phone soak test were written to make that safe. With ten lanes running, machine load reached 50 to 69 on 12 cores, so two vehicle leads were paused and heavy jobs were limited to three at a time. On request, the Syn3h buggy now drives like an electric car: it coasts off throttle, slows itself on the reverse key and parks itself on slopes. At 5:00pm the private previews went down after an access change on the private network.
