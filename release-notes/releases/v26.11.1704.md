## Syn3h v26.11.1704

Quieter bots who rescue each other properly.

Public release at 5:37am Pacific on 17 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

The bots talked too much: well over a hundred lines every ten minutes in a room with four of them, most of it chatter about nothing. Now they talk about a sixth as much, and mostly when something happens. Their rescues were also half-hearted, abandoned on a timer. Now a stuck bot is rescued until it is free, more rescuers join if the first cannot manage, and you can hook on and help.

### Features

- Bots talk about a sixth as much, and mostly when something happens. With one person and four bots, about 16 to 18 lines every ten minutes, down from 108 to 115. Their replies to you are unchanged.
- Rescues work properly: no time limit ends one, more rescuers join if the first cannot free the stuck bot, and if you hook on with your own grapple it counts. Rescue news is told only near a person, with one line for how it ended.
- Wheeled bots wait at the shore instead of following you into lava or deep water.
- A new switch, "Show new messages over the game", keeps chat lines out of the play view. It is off by default on phones.
- A new host setting sets how chatty the bots are.

### Fixes

- A bot counts as stuck after 10 seconds without getting anywhere, so its rescue starts sooner. Hosts can change the wait with the new rescue setting.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ in a browser with a few bots in the room. Drive into the water and watch them stop at the shore; get a bot stuck and watch the others come to tow it out, or hook on with your own grapple.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1704, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Not measured for this release.

### Known issues

- Players on an older build in the same room still see the old long names for the same bots.
- Planets between the three old sizes look wrong to players on frozen older versions.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
- Touch aiming was tested with simulated touches in a phone-sized browser, not yet on a physical phone.
