## Syn3h v26.11.1902

Bots now play the games with you, and dawn fog is lit by the morning.

Public release at 3:16am Pacific on 19 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

Bots used to drive about the world while a game went on around them. Now they play. On the hill they push and sometimes grapple you off. In Tag they cut you off and tag back, with a few Plasma Tag streaks and an occasional Thunder. At most two bots go for any one player, and a Bots setting group adjusts how skilled and how keen they are, starting gentle. And dawn fog now takes the morning light, so it reads as pale, warm mist over the sea and in valleys rather than slate.

### Features

- Bots play King of the Hill: they push, and sometimes grapple, whoever is in the zone.
- Bots play Tag: they head off whoever is "it", and tag back with a few Plasma Tag streaks and an occasional Thunder.
- At most two bots go for any one player.
- New room settings in the Bots group set how skilled and how keen bots are: difficulty, aim, how often they tag, how much of it is Thunder, ramming, cutting you off, and grappling. They start gentle.

### Fixes

- Dawn fog is lit by the morning: pale and warm over the sea and in valleys. Noon is unchanged.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/, start a room with bots, and play King of the Hill or Tag against them.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1902, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Not measured for this release.

### Known issues

- At dawn, fog lies on the sea along coasts.
- Other vehicles do not have headlight beams yet.
- On dry ground, a vehicle left on its roof still rights itself after a few seconds; whether it should is the owner's to decide.
- The pinch zoom was tested with simulated touches, not on a real iPhone.
- How the chat card and the keyboard behave on real phones is not yet confirmed.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
