## Mira v26.11.2601

A new sea, a jet that flies true, a card for every player, and a tidier sidebar.

Public release at 12:00pm Pacific on 26 November 2026, live at https://mira.cc/.

### The story so far

A beta on top of v26.11.2600. The sea was rebuilt, the jet's flight reworked, and every player and bot now has a card of their own. The in-play sidebar was gathered into five cards, every keyboard shortcut is drawn as a key, and several changes stop the game building graphics in the middle of play.

### Features

- The sea has a new surface: sharper ripples near you, glitter in the sun, and calmer patches that fade into sheen with distance.
- The jet flies true: no reverse thrust, steady in the air, a top speed of about 130 m/s at the default feel, and a choice of Gentle or Sharp. It is now 6 m long.
- The jet's thrusters and guards move with your controls.
- Jet wingtip trails start at the wingtips and no longer flash.
- Tap a player or bot in Players to open their card: mute them for yourself, and, as host, mute them for everyone, eject them for a while, or tune a bot's nature.
- The in-play sidebar is now five cards: Game, Players, World, Settings and Chat.
- The lobby has its own scene settings, gathered under one card.
- On a phone the lobby's Settings and Welcome buttons stay stacked at the lower right.
- Every keyboard shortcut in the interface is drawn as a key.
- Control tips now appear as you need them and step aside once you have used a control; chat lines show one at a time in a free spot, and H opens the full list of controls.
- The speed crescent fills to each vehicle's own top speed.
- On an upright phone, the Grapple's two extra buttons sit in a row above the thumb buttons, clear of the minimap.

### Fixes

- Glass text stays readable over bright scenes, including on the lowest graphics settings.
- Welcome's close button and top fade stay put while you scroll.
- Changing quality keeps its cover until the new level is ready, and other vehicles' looks are prepared during a climb, so play no longer pauses to build them.
- The planet names in the lobby return to their quieter look, and move with eased motion instead of flashing in and out.
- The lobby no longer draws the hidden world behind it, and switching worlds stays covered until the new one is ready.
- A jet bot holding its position settles there instead of drifting.
- Plasma Thunder no longer detonates under water or lava; it fizzles at the surface.

### Play

Open https://mira.cc/ and play as usual.

This exact version stays playable at https://mira.cc/?v=26.11.2601, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

The new sea's cost, measured with GPU timestamps on WebGL 2 against v26.11.2500: unchanged at Minimal and Low, about 0.5 ms more at Medium, 1.4 to 1.9 ms more at High, and more again at Ultra and Cinematic. It builds nothing in play.

### Known issues

- The frame probe and the three new test links have not yet been run on a real iPhone.
- Record blank frames cannot see a flash in a scene that is already nearly all black, a black area under about a third of the screen, or anything Safari does after the game hands the frame over. It has not been verified in Safari.
- The cause of the black flashes with sun shadows on is not yet fixed, only avoided by turning the shadows off.
- On the same devices water refraction, the force field contact glow and the headlight beams' soft edge are still off.
- The graphics process can still stop on iPhone.
- On an older page still open in a room, the room's gear looks replace that player's own.
- On a sideways phone the carousel's next picture passes under the Settings and Welcome buttons.
- On WebGL 2 the menu can pause for up to about 2.5 s while quality climbs from Low to Medium.
- Quality changes to Ultra and Cinematic work as before.
- Under-water ripple needs WebGPU; on WebGL 2 the water looks as before.
- Its cost on a phone has not been measured; the copied diagnostics report says whether it is on.
- After the quality climbs from Low to Medium, a bot's shadow can build once.
- After a climb to Ultra or Cinematic, a bot vehicle's mid-distance model can occasionally build once during play.
- A Tag beam used under water still draws over the surface.
- Not yet tested in Safari with WebGPU.
- Which side of a sideways iPhone has the camera cutout is worked out from the screen's rotation and has not been checked on a real iPhone.
- At Tint settings below a style's default, text can be pale over bright scenes; that is the player's choice.
- The Glass and Frost styles are removed; a saved choice of either becomes Flat.
- For the first instant, the burst's streaks still cross most of the screen.
- A player still on a version before v26.11.2118 who pauses is treated as hidden, so the host role passes after 3 seconds instead of 20.
- Liquid has not been timed on any phone.
- Entering a world at Ultra or Cinematic, the first automatic drop can still hitch.
- On the older graphics path, choosing a top quality level before pressing Play makes entering a world slower.
- On the older graphics path, changing vehicle at Low can cause a small hitch.
- The in-game performance check that found this has not yet been run for the jet and the buggy at their own detail-switch distances.
- No phone has been measured for these quality steps.
- The sun rays' GPU cost was not measured, and whether they flicker while driving was judged by eye only.
- Changing the quality level in play rebuilds the firework batches.
- A player still on the previous version sees Plasma Thunder as it was and ignores the new messages. Their version never reports where its bolts end, so players on this version burst those bolts on their own after 1.5 s, and those bursts push nobody.
- The first touch on a phone builds one effect pipeline.
- On WebGL 2, preparation spends about 3 s waiting between completion checks.
- Not measured on a physical iPhone; touch was emulated on a Mac.
- Phone behaviour was tested in browser engines on a Mac, not on a physical iPhone.
- A player on an older version whose cord holds someone cannot be unhooked by the new stop words; stretching the cord still frees them.
- At dawn, fog lies on the sea along coasts.
- On dry ground, a vehicle left on its roof still rights itself after a few seconds; whether it should is the owner's to decide.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
