## Syn3h v26.11.1712

A one-piece driving column on the left, new touch controls, chat as a card, and a minimap that turns with you.

Public release at 8:00pm Pacific on 17 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

These are design changes, shipped so the owner can adjust them on the live game. Chat used to float over the game; it is now the last card in the right panel, called Chat everywhere. The panel's top row is now just the search field and two glowing icons, Pause and Leave world, and on phones one card is open at a time. The minimap now turns with the camera, so the way you face is always up. The left side of the driving HUD is now one instrument, and on phones Tag becomes an aiming stick.

### Features

- Chat is now a card at the bottom of the right panel, called Chat everywhere. Y opens it and puts you in the message field; typing in chat frees the cursor.
- The minimap turns with the camera, heading up, and N travels round its rim. A new setting, Minimap north up, keeps north at the top instead.
- The left side of the driving HUD is one instrument: gear, then your vehicle's switches (Wings, Flight, Zorb), then Chat, then the minimap. A dot on its spine lights for whatever is selected or on. No more outlined pills.
- Key hints show only while the mouse is free; while you drive, the column shows just names and what is on. Keys are shown the same way everywhere: bright letters, no boxes, in the column, the controls card and the H help.
- Pick gear with the number keys, or a click or tap. T no longer cycles gear.
- Chat has its own row in the column, showing how many lines you have not read; it opens the Chat card.
- Phones: press and drag TAG to aim it like a second stick, the thumb buttons form one even grid, and two fingers pinch to zoom the orbit camera.
- Phones and tablets: the minimap stays in the lower left, as on a computer, and you can start the drive stick right on top of it.
- The right panel's top row is the search field plus glowing Pause and Leave world icons, with no box around them and no world name. Leave world still asks first.

### Fixes

- Gear names are never cut off, at any size.
- On a phone held sideways, the jet's throttle no longer sits under the Panels tab, and on an iPad the Panels tab no longer covers the King of the Hill winner.
- On phones one card is open at a time, and the card you tapped stays under your finger.
- Players and session is a card like the others, and open cards stay open across reloads and when the panel is closed and reopened.
- Setting names wrap instead of being cut off with an ellipsis, and an open card's header stays solid while you scroll.
- Opening an address with a doubled slash (such as syn3h.com//) now loads the game instead of failing at startup.
- The game's sound now starts from silence, so it cannot sound at full volume for a moment before fading in.
- On a browser or device with no 3D graphics the game can use, Syn3h now says so plainly and suggests turning on hardware acceleration, trying a current browser or another device, instead of showing a developer error and a Reload button that cannot help. Other start failures say that reloading often fixes it.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/, enter a world, press Y to chat, and turn the camera to watch the minimap turn with you.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1712, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

The minimap redraws its marks every 3 degrees of turn or every 100 ms while the camera turns, about 0.1 ms a frame, and nothing extra when it holds still.

### Known issues

- These design changes are shipped for the owner to adjust on the live game.
- How the chat card and the keyboard behave on real phones is not yet confirmed; it was tested in phone-sized browsers.
- On a slow phone the lobby may draw slightly slower with the new touch controls; this is being measured.
- Touch aiming was tested with simulated touches in phone-sized browsers, not yet on a physical phone.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
