## Syn3h v26.11.1712

Chat as a card in the side panel, a cleaner top row, and a minimap that turns with you.

Public release at 8:00pm Pacific on 17 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

These are design changes, shipped so the owner can adjust them on the live game. Chat used to float over the game; it is now the last card in the right panel, called Chat everywhere. The panel's top row is now just the search field and two glowing icons, Pause and Leave world, and on phones one card is open at a time. The minimap now turns with the camera, so the way you face is always up.

### Features

- Chat is now a card at the bottom of the right panel, called Chat everywhere. Y opens it and puts you in the message field; typing in chat frees the cursor.
- The minimap turns with the camera, heading up, and N travels round its rim. A new setting, Minimap north up, keeps north at the top instead.
- The right panel's top row is the search field plus glowing Pause and Leave world icons, with no box around them and no world name. Leave world still asks first.

### Fixes

- On phones one card is open at a time, and the card you tapped stays under your finger.
- Players and session is a card like the others, and open cards stay open across reloads and when the panel is closed and reopened.
- Setting names wrap instead of being cut off with an ellipsis, and an open card's header stays solid while you scroll.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/, enter a world, press Y to chat, and turn the camera to watch the minimap turn with you.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1712, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

The minimap redraws its marks every 3 degrees of turn or every 100 ms while the camera turns, about 0.1 ms a frame, and nothing extra when it holds still.

### Known issues

- These design changes are shipped for the owner to adjust on the live game.
- How the chat card and the keyboard behave on real phones is not yet confirmed; it was tested in phone-sized browsers.
- On a phone held sideways, the jet's throttle-down button overlaps the navigation buttons. The fix is on the touch controls branch.
- On an iPad, the navigation buttons overlap the King of the Hill winner announcement.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
