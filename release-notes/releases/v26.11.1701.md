## Syn3h v26.11.1701

A Big Bang to open the galaxy, a calmer loading view, and a simpler sidebar.

Public release at 4:00am Pacific on 17 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

The galaxy now begins with a bang. Every load opens on a pulsing point of light, which pops into stars, sun rays and planets that glide clockwise onto their orbits, while the Syn3h name fades in. The loading view stays quiet for its first three seconds and only then says what it is doing. Planets show their night sides, softly lit, so their surfaces read as the view swings past. The sidebar lost its clutter: its foot is just Leave world, and the lobby no longer has a Multiplayer button, since everything it held is in play.

### Features

- Opening: every load opens with a small cartoon Big Bang. A pulsing point of light while loading, a pop, then stars, rays and planets unfolding over about four and a half seconds, the planets gliding clockwise onto their orbits. Any input skips it, and there is no full-screen flash.
- The Syn3h name starts fading in the moment the lobby is drawn and takes 4.2 seconds on a smooth curve. Before, it stayed dark for 7 seconds and then snapped in. The same curve plays on a return from a world.
- Loading: only the point of light for the first 3 seconds; then a playful line and a progress line, reading like "56 s · 6.2 MB", fade in together. Fast loads never show them.
- The lobby sun's rays are at half their former strength; its core, flare and the light on the Syn3h name are unchanged.
- The night side of each lobby planet gets a soft cool fill, so surface detail reads on the side facing the camera. Day sides are unchanged.
- Sidebar: its foot is just Leave world. Copy, Paste and Reset sit at the top of the World card, rows no longer draw a box inside a card, and the lobby's settings cards sit directly over the lobby.
- The lobby has no Multiplayer button. The game mode, the invite link and QR code, and the scoreboard are all in play: the Game card, Players and session, and J.

### Fixes

- A tooltip now closes with its card.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ in a browser and watch the galaxy bang into being, or press any key to skip it. Swing the view round to see the planets' night sides, then tap a world. In play, press J to invite someone.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1701, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Not measured for this release. On the builder's runs the split start-up code added 0.45 percent to the JavaScript shipped, and the first lobby frame arrived as before, unthrottled and on a slow 4G connection.

### Known issues

- Players on an older build in the same room still see the old long names for the same bots.
- Planets between the three old sizes look wrong to players on frozen older versions.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
- Touch aiming was tested with simulated touches in a phone-sized browser, not yet on a physical phone.
