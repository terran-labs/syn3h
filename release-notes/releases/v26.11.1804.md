## Syn3h v26.11.1804

Softer buggy headlights, a horizon that blends gently into the sky's haze, and rivers that float vehicles exactly where they bend into pools.

Public release at 8:00pm Pacific on 18 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

At night the buggy's headlights blew the view in front of it out to white. They are now about half as bright, with a setting to adjust them. And on the small planets, far land used to end against the sky in a hard line; the horizon now fades softly into the same haze the land takes. That change is subtle: it is meant to be barely noticeable. Where a river bends into a pool, vehicles now float exactly on the water you see, and the minimap and the grass on the banks follow the river as it is drawn.

### Features

- A new setting, "Buggy headlights (1 is the old brightness)", scales the buggy's two road lights and its lens glow. It starts at 0.4.

### Fixes

- The buggy's headlights are about half as bright, so the night view in front of a buggy no longer blows out to white.
- Where a river bends into a pool, vehicles now float exactly on the drawn water.
- The minimap and the grass along river banks follow the river as it is drawn.
- The horizon blends more softly into the sky's haze, instead of ending in a hard line. The change is subtle; a new Weather setting, "Haze at the sky's horizon", sets how much.

### Handling

- On a river's bends into pools, vehicles ride the drawn water, so their height there changes where the old surface sat above or below it.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/, drive the buggy at night, then look out at the horizon on a small planet.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1804, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Not measured for this release.

### Known issues

- Desert rivers stay a pale mint, from that world's palette.
- On phones at Low and Medium, lakes and rivers are drawn flatter than the waves vehicles ride, by up to about a quarter of a metre.
- How the chat card and the keyboard behave on real phones is not yet confirmed.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
