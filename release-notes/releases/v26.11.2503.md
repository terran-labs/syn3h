## Mira v26.11.2503

Vehicles skip across water, Welcome opens on its first line, and every setting can go back to its default.

Public release at 10:00pm Pacific on 25 November 2026, live at https://mira.cc/.

### The story so far

The third beta. Light, wide vehicles now plane and skip across water, and the wakes behind them are easier to see. The Welcome guide lost its tabs and title and gained a newsletter line, and every setting gained a reset to its default. The version label now opens Updates at once, which ends the second beta's half-second wait. The dev server's release list also stopped holding up the game's own checks.

### Features

- Light, wide vehicles skim and skip across water at speed, with a splash and a sound at each touchdown. Heavy ones do not, and the tank never skips.
- Wakes behind vehicles on water are stronger, wider and last longer.
- Welcome opens on "Welcome to Mira, a sci-fi wonderland of tiny worlds to explore with your friends." with no tabs or title bar. In play, opening it pauses you, and closing it resumes play unless you had paused first.
- A line near the end of Welcome links to the Mira newsletter.
- Every setting has a reset arrow that brings back its default, and each section and card can reset all of its changes at once.
- The share card shows the Mira mark with its gold glow and "Your galaxy awaits."

### Fixes

- The version label opens Updates at once, by tap, click or keyboard.
- Moving between worlds shows only the move's own loading lines.
- On phones, a day's release count stays on the same line as its date in Updates.
- The minimap's arrows no longer have a dark halo under them.
- The jet's help says Space switches between hover and cruise.

### Handling

Vehicles that touch water now share one planing contact: lift grows with speed over each vehicle's own planing speed, so a light, wide hull skims and a heavy one does not. The skip's friction and how much speed a skip keeps are the owner's tuning after playing it.

### Play

Open https://mira.cc/ and play as usual.

This exact version stays playable at https://mira.cc/?v=26.11.2503, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Frame cost: not measured for v26.11.2502.
