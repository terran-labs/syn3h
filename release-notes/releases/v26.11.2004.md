## Syn3h v26.11.2004

Held charges start on time, and matching beams in the mist.

Public release at 3:30am Pacific on 20 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

Small fixes. If you keep holding through a cooldown, the next charge now starts the moment it is ready instead of waiting for you to press again. The hovercraft and jet beams in mist were brighter than the buggy's; they now match. And a request for a file that does not exist gets its answer quickly.

### Features

No new features in this release.

### Fixes

- Holding through a cooldown starts the next charge the moment it is ready.
- Hovercraft and jet headlight beams in mist now match the buggy's brightness. Three Graphics settings tune them.
- A request for a missing file is answered quickly, instead of after a few seconds.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/, hold through a cooldown and watch the next charge begin. Drive the hovercraft at dawn beside a buggy.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.2004, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

The first Plasma Thunder burst builds no new shaders on both graphics paths, WebGPU and WebGL 2. The dimmer beams change only brightness, so nothing new is built in play.

### Known issues

- Changing the quality level in play rebuilds the firework batches.
- A player still on the previous version sees Plasma Thunder as it was and ignores the new messages. Their version never reports where its bolts end, so players on this version burst those bolts on their own after 1.5 s, and those bursts push nobody.
- Changing the quality level rebuilds 24 to 51 materials.
- Entering a world again repeats the preparation.
- The first touch on a phone builds one effect pipeline.
- On WebGL 2, preparation spends about 3 s waiting between completion checks.
- Not measured on a physical iPhone; touch was emulated on a Mac.
- The glass is a CSS imitation; a closer match to Apple's is in progress.
- Phone behaviour was tested in browser engines on a Mac, not on a physical iPhone.
- A player on an older version whose cord holds someone cannot be unhooked by the new stop words; stretching the cord still frees them.
- At dawn, fog lies on the sea along coasts.
- Bots' vehicles do not show headlight beams in mist yet.
- On dry ground, a vehicle left on its roof still rights itself after a few seconds; whether it should is the owner's to decide.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
