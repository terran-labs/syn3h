### The story so far

Four builders worked in parallel for about twenty minutes, and their branches merged into the first playable build. The buggy's driving physics were ported from the original source: one rigid body, four raycasts and hand applied forces for the motor, friction, suspension, four wheel steering and self righting. The terrain came from a seeded heightfield generator with dunes, mesas and a volcano, built identically on every machine from one number. Multiplayer arrived in the same merge: a room server that runs locally or as one Cloudflare Durable Object per room, nearby remote vehicles kept as physical bodies so they can shove each other, a tag game, and sliders shared across the room with a host lock. The original vehicle models, music and sound effects were converted and added, and a private preview link went up a few minutes later.

### Features

- The ported buggy with its original suspension, motor, friction, steering and self righting
- A generated landscape from a shared seed, with one physics collider for the whole heightfield
- Multiplayer rooms with smooth playback of other players, ramming, a tag game and a host lock on shared sliders
- The original vehicle models, music and sound effects

### Known issues

- The ported buggy did not feel right; the next build changed course
- The world is open country rather than an island, and lava is not drawn yet
