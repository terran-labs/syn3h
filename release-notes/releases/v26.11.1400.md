### The story so far

The project began on Saturday afternoon with research and a written plan, and no game code. The plan set the weekend's goal: satisfying off road sci fi vehicle physics, lighting that is never baked so that alien sunrises and sunsets can be generated, and basic multiplayer, so a few friends can drive to the top of a volcano and play King of the Hill. Reading the original source showed that each of the old vehicles was one rigid body with raycasts and hand applied forces, so their handling could be ported nearly line for line instead of reinvented. It also asked for as many physics, vehicle and world sliders as possible, shared live by everyone in the world, with a host lock. The roadmap ordered the work so that every step ended in something playable, and said the buggy on a volcano under a live sky was worth having even if nothing else was finished.

### Features

- A proposal: Three.js with the WebGPU renderer and a WebGL 2 fallback, Rapier physics at a fixed 50 Hz, a small WebSocket room server, and one typed parameter registry behind every slider
- A roadmap of seven playable steps for the weekend: foundation, a volcano under an alien sky, the buggy, multiplayer and shared sliders, more vehicles, King of the Hill, then sound and the original look
- Architecture notes on how the original vehicles, network model and settings worked, and research on reference games and on real world cities as a stretch goal
