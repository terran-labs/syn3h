## Syn3h v26.11.1511

The galaxy lobby stays on screen while idle.

Public release at 3:17pm Pacific on 15 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

The owner requested an immediate fix after the idle volcano tour interrupted an iPad demo. This release deletes that tour and its idle trigger. Loading leads to the galaxy, which stays visible until you choose a planet.

### Features

- The solar-system lobby remains the entry screen after loading, including when nobody touches the screen.

### Fixes

- Removed the idle volcano tour, its overlay, styles and automatic activation. The old attract query parameter no longer starts it.
- Preserved the cinematic camera connection used for the descent into a selected planet.
