### The story so far

Release 2 was cut from the m14 tag on its own branch and took only cherry picked fixes, so trunk could keep carrying features nobody had seen yet. The order of importance set for it was the iPad black screen, the wing lift fixes, the rear-end shove and share tags on www. The iPad black screen turned out to be a wait of about half a minute for the first frame over the development preview, which sends about 28 MB; production sends about 2 MB and starts in 1.4 to 2.9 seconds. A different black screen was found in Safari: the jet handed the renderer a texture whose image had not arrived yet, which the Chrome smoke tests had never shown, so every release is now checked in WebKit as well as Chrome. The generated adaptive score was retuned and heard, and stayed a choice rather than the default because the owner wanted it better first. This release went live at 8:07pm.

### Features

- Force field wings lift a buggy decisively out of lava and water; a fast wings-open touch on water skips, and holding forward at escape height launches the buggy into flight
- In multiplayer, a rear-end ram now pushes the car in front on that player's own screen too, and the push is felt once
- The Syn3h buggy gains wing struts and hub motor stators, which glow in the accent colour and spark with motor effort, and lamps of its own

### Fixes

- The jet no longer shows a black 3D view in Safari while one of its textures is still downloading
- With the forward key held, the buggy always drives forward; it no longer rolls back downhill with its wheels turning backward
- Tag beams stay green at night instead of bleaching to white
- The jet's controls lead with Hold R to lift off, and a pilot who pushes W on a parked jet is reminded
- Joining a room now takes the room's whole world, so two players can no longer build different islands from one room
- Players who share a name get numbers, the tag passes on from a player who has gone quiet for eight seconds, and silent connections close after three minutes
- Remote vehicles are shown with at most 150 ms of delay, so they no longer trail a third of a second behind on a lossy connection
- Link previews show the game's card image, addressed on www.syn3h.com
- On a tablet held sideways, the touch buttons sit clear of the minimap
- The lobby is lighter on phones and at the lowest graphics level, and a guest who arrives by a link to a world stays on that world until they join
- The ride view hides only the parts of a vehicle it marks, so drivers see their own body around them
- The alpine world has knife ridges, a horn peak with pinnacles round its summit tarn, wild flowers, more snow and denser forest
- A new loading screen covers the page until the world is ready
- The generated adaptive score can be chosen as the music; the recorded track stays the default

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ in a browser, choose a vehicle and press Play. Press H in game for every control.

- Buggies: W A S D or the arrow keys to drive, Shift to brake, Space for force field wings, L or click for a tag beam
- Jet: hold R to lift off and climb, F to come down, W and S to tip the nose, A and D to bank, Space to switch between hover and flight
- Hovercraft: W for thrust, A and D to turn, Shift to brake
- Camera: C cycles chase, cockpit, orbit, stationary and roam
- On a phone or tablet: the left thumb drives and steers, with WINGS, BRAKE and TAG buttons; on the jet hold THR + to lift off
- If the 3D view stays blank, `?webgl=1` forces WebGL and `?diag=1` shows render diagnostics

### Known issues

- syn3h.com without www reaches the game through a redirect on the old host; www.syn3h.com is served directly
- In one two-player check made within a minute of this deploy, a slider change reached one player but not the other; three reruns passed. It may affect a room open during a deploy
- Bots reach every summit, then ram each other, and some drive off the edge
- Changing vehicle in the lobby reloads the page
- The loading screen is a dim ring with no words
