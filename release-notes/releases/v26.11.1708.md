## Syn3h v26.11.1708

Entering a planet no longer freezes the game, leaving climbs out smoothly, bots arrive one by one, and bot races start for a host.

Public release at 2:00pm Pacific on 17 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

Clicking a planet used to stall the game for nearly half a second while the world was built. The world is now prepared behind the galaxy's dive: its heights come from a background worker and its meshes are rebuilt a piece at a time, so the dive keeps moving. Leaving a world climbs back out of it before the galaxy eases home. Bots no longer all appear in one frame; they arrive one at a time near the player, and guests see them arrive the same way. Bot races, which never started for a host with automatic races on, now do.

### Features

- Leaving a world climbs back out of it, then the galaxy eases home, in about 2.5 seconds instead of a cut straight to the lobby.
- Bots arrive one at a time near the player, in the arrival cloud, an interval apart. Guests see the host's bots arrive the same way.
- After a race, the race panel says whether you won, finished or did not finish, instead of still saying "Racing".

### Fixes

- Clicking a planet no longer freezes the game. Measured at High against v26.11.1706, the worst frame from the click to driving fell from 433 ms to 150 ms, and frames over 50 ms from 11 to 2. Medium on a phone-sized view improved as much.
- If preparing a world runs long, the dive holds in the air for at most 15 seconds, then lands with whatever is ready.
- If the background worker cannot load or does not answer, the world is generated the old way instead.
- Bot races now start for a host when automatic races are on. They never did before.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/, click a planet and watch the dive, then leave the world and watch the climb back out. Turn on automatic races in a room with bots to race them.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1708, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Entering a world at High, against v26.11.1706, five runs a side: worst frame 150 ms against 433 ms; frames over 50 ms, 2 against 11. Leaving a world: level with before, 66 ms worst against 67 ms. Ultra was not measured.

### Known issues

- A bot is sent away again if it measurably slowed the host's game, so a slow host device now means fewer bots in the room for everyone.
- Bots never arrive on a page running below about 3 frames a second.
- On 120 Hz displays a bot can occasionally be judged too costly and sent away when the machine is busy.
- In a race, a bot may not reach a far summit in time, and an idle player can be counted as having left the race. Both are being looked into.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- On a phone held sideways, the jet's throttle-down button overlaps the navigation buttons. The fix is on the touch controls branch.
- On an iPad, the navigation buttons overlap the King of the Hill winner announcement.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
