## Syn3h v26.11.1708

A black-and-grey interface, new touch controls with an aiming stick, and brighter planet names in the lobby.

Public release at 2:00pm Pacific on 17 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

These are design changes, shipped so the owner can adjust them on the live game rather than in a preview. The interface loses its blue tint: panels, plates and the sidebar are now near-black glass with grey lines and white lettering, and anything switched on shows in plain white. On a phone, Tag becomes a stick you can aim with, every thumb button is the same size on two even rows, and two fingers pinch to zoom. In the lobby, planet names are brighter and take on their planet's glow when you point at them.

### Features

- Touch: press and drag TAG to aim, like a second stick. Plasma Tag keeps tagging while you hold it; other gear goes where the centre reticle points when you let go, and dragging onto Cancel sends nothing.
- Touch: TAG, WINGS and BRAKE swap places, and every thumb button is one size on two evenly spaced rows, mirrored for left-handed play. The hovercraft, which has no wings, leaves that slot empty so TAG sits in the same place on every vehicle.
- Touch: two fingers on the right side of the screen pinch to zoom the orbit camera.
- Lobby planet names are brighter and glow in their planet's colour when you point at them.

### Fixes

- The interface's panels, sidebar, Me card, About and Updates are black and grey glass instead of blue, with neutral white for anything switched on.
- On a phone held sideways, the jet's throttle-down button no longer covers the navigation buttons.
- The Panels tab on a phone stands clear of the thumb buttons, and steps aside while a match announcement shows.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release. Tag's aiming speed, dead zone and curve are new settings in the parameter registry.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ on a phone, choose a vehicle and try aiming with the TAG stick; on a computer, look at the new black-and-grey panels.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1708, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Not measured for this release.

### Known issues

- These design changes are shipped for the owner to adjust on the live game; expect further changes to colours and layout.
- Touch aiming was tested with simulated touches in a phone-sized browser, not yet on a physical phone.
- The touch stick and thumb buttons keep their blue fill for now; only their lettering went neutral.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Players on an older build in the same room still see the old long names for the same bots.
- Planets between the three old sizes look wrong to players on frozen older versions.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
