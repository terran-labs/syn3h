## Syn3h v26.11.1501

Clouds with depth, smoother edges and saved worlds in the galaxy.

Public release at 3:38am Pacific on 15 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

Release 4 enabled the new vehicles, water effects, tools and lighting in ordinary play.
This update adds cloud volumes and temporal antialiasing at full quality, plus local
bookmarks that appear as moons beside their planets. Lower quality settings retain their
fallback rendering, and hosts keep their existing world permissions.

### Features

**Clouds and edges**

Procedural clouds have volume and follow the world's weather. Their density repeats
across the wrapping world, so crossing a seam does not jump to a different cloud pattern.
Reflections retain the sky's cloud layer when the camera uses cloud volumes.

Temporal antialiasing combines eligible pixels across frames. Camera cuts, world changes
and wrapping reset its history. Water waves, grass, particles and other shader-positioned
surfaces use the current frame to avoid incorrect motion trails.

**Saved-world moons**

Save up to 12 worlds in this browser from the World card. Each bookmark keeps the world's
settings, including its seed and overrides. Tap a saved moon to descend into that world,
or choose it from Worlds. Deleting a bookmark only removes the local save.

Guests can save settings, but restoring them still respects the current room's authority.
If the room's authoritative settings replace a bookmark, the interface reports that outcome.
Bookmarks do not store room names or player identities.

### Fixes

The bottom-left minimap now sits above the joystick on wide touch screens as well as
phones and short landscape layouts. Saving from the inspector shows visible feedback;
Enter and Space activate Save without triggering a tool or changing vehicle mode.

### Play

Open the World card and choose Save current world. Return to the galaxy through Exit
world to find its moon. Cloud volumes and temporal antialiasing work on ordinary entry
at full quality, with off controls and lower-quality fallbacks preserved.

### Known issues

Physical phone and iPad performance remains unmeasured. Clouds do not cast shadows on
the ground. Screen-space indirect light cannot include surfaces outside the view, and
water remains opaque from above. Aircraft Pester avoidance is unfinished.

Saved worlds are local to this browser. Current room authority and future generator
changes can affect the restored result. The room-join notification can still overlap
the heading briefly.
