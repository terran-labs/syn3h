## Syn3h v26.11.2002

Nothing changes in play; releases are faster and old versions are stored once.

Public release at 3:00am Pacific on 20 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

Every earlier version of Syn3h stays playable at its own address. Until now, each release carried a full copy of every earlier version with it, so each release grew and took longer. Now the earlier versions are stored once, and each release carries only itself. Nothing changes in play.

### Features

No new features in this release.

### Fixes

- Releases no longer carry a copy of every earlier version, so they are faster to make.
- Earlier versions are stored once and stay playable at their own addresses.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ and play as usual. An earlier version, such as https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1503, still opens at its own address.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.2002, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Not measured for players; nothing they load has changed.

### Known issues

- Changing the quality level rebuilds 24 to 51 materials.
- Entering a world again repeats the preparation.
- The first touch on a phone builds one effect pipeline.
- On WebGL 2, preparation spends about 3 s waiting between completion checks.
- Not measured on a physical iPhone; touch was emulated on a Mac.
- The glass is a CSS imitation; a closer match to Apple's is in progress.
- Phone behaviour was tested in browser engines on a Mac, not on a physical iPhone.
- A player on an older version whose cord holds someone cannot be unhooked by the new stop words; stretching the cord still frees them.
- At dawn, fog lies on the sea along coasts.
- Bots' vehicles do not show headlight beams in mist yet.
- On dry ground, a vehicle left on its roof still rights itself after a few seconds; whether it should is the owner's to decide.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
