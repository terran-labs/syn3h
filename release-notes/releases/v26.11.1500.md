## Syn3h v26.11.1500

Round worlds, new vehicles and shared tools, ready on ordinary entry.

Public release at 3:05am Pacific on 15 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

This release brings the completed worlds, vehicles, tools and lighting into ordinary play.
Combined testing found water-contact and jet-pilot defects, which were corrected before
release. The first public attempt failed a bot check and was rolled back. The retry used
fresh test rooms and passed public multiplayer and all eight vehicle checks; the earlier
failure's cause remains unproven.

### Features

Choose a planet in the galaxy and descend straight into play. Explore nine wrapping worlds. Worlds with air have wind and haze; the airless Moon has neither.
Richer ground surfaces and short procedural vegetation follow climate and quality tiers. Custom world editing keeps the map visible while sliders change it.
Galaxy population lights show connected players across rooms without exposing room names.

The Syn3h buggy has its new cockpit, force-field canopy, smooth tyres and distance models.
The jet uses the Hexring Mk2 body. Zorb mode puts the vehicle inside a rolling force-field
ball. Four mixed bots start by default, subject to the device's quality budget; Daydream
takes over after five idle minutes. Nearby eligible ground bots can start landmark races.

**Water, light and sound**

Waves, currents, sinking, underwater effects, ripples and spray are enabled by default.
Wing skims retain their speed, and Zorb keeps its shallow support depth. Worlds with lava
keep lava, and dry worlds remain dry.

Lighting includes softer sun shadows, corrected ambient occlusion, two buggy headlights
and screen-space indirect light at full quality. Recorded music starts by default, with
the adaptive score available to select. Wind sound is generated; supplied music and tag
beam sounds remain.

**Shared tools**

Bungee, Yoink, Big Red and Pesters are enabled alongside tag beams. Bots can use the tools;
wheeled bots can dodge Pesters. Hosts retain off controls. Brief disconnects can restore
the same player, while stored room opt-outs remain respected.

### Fixes

Jet pilots now use the correct inertia frame and limit descent lean. Removed bot leaders
no longer cause a stale physics read. Water contact correctly reaches motor damping.
Network tool checks bound range, traffic and incoming effects.

### Play

Open the configuration rail for World, Sky, Game, Vehicles and personal settings.
Use Exit world to return to the galaxy. Press T to cycle available tools and X for Zorb.

### Known issues

Physical phone and iPad performance and audio listening still need hands-on review.
Touch jet controls can overlap the minimap on wider screens; a correction is queued.
Water is still opaque from above. Aircraft Pester evasion is unfinished. Screen-space
indirect light cannot include surfaces outside the view. Volumetric clouds, temporal
antialiasing and saved-world moons are not in this release.
