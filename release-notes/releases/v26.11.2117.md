## Syn3h v26.11.2117

Clearer touch controls on iPad and phone, and glass that reads well.

Public release at 6:10pm Pacific on 21 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

This release tidies the interface on touch screens, especially the iPad. The Panels button now sits above Tag and Wings on every touch device instead of a tab on the edge, and the gear selector is a compact strip at the top left that stays clear of the speed and heading. Open panels keep one even margin and one corner shape, without a dark title bar, and their text stays readable over a bright sky. In the lobby, the Welcome guide has a carousel, the sun is dimmed behind it, and the version number opens Updates. The Glass setting's Liquid look now covers every button and stays in place when post effects are switched off.

### Features

- The Welcome guide shows its pictures in a carousel, with one Close control, and the sun dims behind it.

### Fixes

- On every touch device the Panels button sits above Tag and Wings; the rotated tab on the edge is gone.
- The gear selector is a compact strip at the top left, clear of the speed and heading readouts; charge bars sit under the gear's name.
- Open panels keep one margin and one corner shape, with no darker title bar, and their text stays readable over a bright sky.
- In the lobby, the in-game Panels button no longer appears, the version number opens Updates, and the Settings card runs the full height.
- With Liquid chosen, every button is drawn in glass, and the controls stay in glass when post effects are switched off.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ on an iPad or a phone, enter a sunny world, and open the panels. Try each stop of Settings, Glass.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.2117, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

At the default Glass look, entering a world builds the same shaders as before, and quality steps still build nothing in play.

### Known issues

- At Liquid the gear selector is shown as glass pills; at the other looks it is bare names.
- Planet captions can still draw over the lobby buttons.
- Liquid has not been timed on any phone.
- Entering a world at Ultra or Cinematic, the first automatic drop can still hitch.
- On the older graphics path, choosing a top quality level before pressing Play makes entering a world slower.
- On the older graphics path, changing vehicle at Low can cause a small hitch.
- The in-game performance check that found this has not yet been run for the jet and the buggy at their own detail-switch distances.
- No phone has been measured for these quality steps.
- The sun rays' GPU cost was not measured, and whether they flicker while driving was judged by eye only.
- Changing the quality level in play rebuilds the firework batches.
- A player still on the previous version sees Plasma Thunder as it was and ignores the new messages. Their version never reports where its bolts end, so players on this version burst those bolts on their own after 1.5 s, and those bursts push nobody.
- The first touch on a phone builds one effect pipeline.
- On WebGL 2, preparation spends about 3 s waiting between completion checks.
- Not measured on a physical iPhone; touch was emulated on a Mac.
- Phone behaviour was tested in browser engines on a Mac, not on a physical iPhone.
- A player on an older version whose cord holds someone cannot be unhooked by the new stop words; stretching the cord still frees them.
- At dawn, fog lies on the sea along coasts.
- Bots' vehicles do not show headlight beams in mist yet.
- On dry ground, a vehicle left on its roof still rights itself after a few seconds; whether it should is the owner's to decide.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
