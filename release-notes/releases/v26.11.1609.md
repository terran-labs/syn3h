## Syn3h v26.11.1609

Your own name and colours, clearer switches, a photo mode and monster-truck tyres.

Public release at 6:39pm Pacific on 16 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

The Me card now holds who you are: pick a vehicle, set your colours or roll random ones, and choose a name that everyone in the room sees change as you type it. Every on/off setting has become one clear switch, the Performance card can set the render resolution, and the I key cycles the interface down to nothing for photos. The buggy gained a Tyre size slider, the world settings name their biomes, and the sea now rises and falls smoothly. Vehicle labels now name the car straight ahead and carry each vehicle's own paint; more work on the labels is coming.

### Features

- Me card: a Buggy, Jet or Hovercraft picker; body and accent colours on their own line with a Randomize button that eases over a second; and your player name, editable, with a dice button. Your name is kept on this device and changes live for everyone in the room. Generated names for people and bots draw on a much larger vocabulary.
- Switches: every on/off control is one unmistakable switch: the knob to the right on bright cyan with the word "on", to the left on a dim track with "off", and a padlock when the host has locked it.
- Render resolution: a slider on the Performance card shows the percentage and the pixel size, with an Auto button.
- Sidebar cards no longer squeeze each other: the card open longest closes to make room.
- Interface: Full, Driving or Hidden, cycled with the I key. Hidden is a photo mode; Escape always brings the interface back. It has no effect on touch screens.
- Tyre size: a slider in the Buggy settings from 0.6 to 3 times. The wheels really change: radius, ride height, width and wheelbase follow, top speed does not, and other players see it. At the biggest size the buggy rolls over at full lock above about 30 m/s.
- World settings: biomes are now called biomes, with named buttons, and the swamp world is Mire. Changing the sea level now glides instead of jumping. Planets are a little rounder by default.
- Vehicle labels now name the car straight ahead, and each label carries its vehicle's own paint: a larger, brighter plate for people and a smaller, quieter one for bots.
- The tank is set aside for now; a saved or linked tank becomes a buggy.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ in a browser. Drag to look around the galaxy and tap a world to go there. Open the Me card in the sidebar to change vehicle, colours or name; press I to change how much of the interface you see.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1609, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Not measured for this release.

### Known issues

- Vehicle labels still slide and draw leader lines, can hide behind your own wings and are hard to read from far away; a steadier, lighter version is in progress.
- A gamepad button held down while the About guide is open takes effect when the guide closes.
- Touch aiming was tested with simulated touches in a phone-sized browser, not yet on a physical phone.
