A simpler sidebar and trails at the Buggy’s wing tips.

### The story so far

### Features

- Vehicle selection and body and accent colours live at the top of Vehicles. The redundant quick row and sidebar header are removed.
- Cards run in this order: Game, Players, Vehicles, World, Sky, Performance and You. Search, Pause and world actions stay at the bottom.

### Fixes

- The sidebar slides and fades with cursor capture, respects reduced-motion settings, and retains the Panels toggle on touch devices.
- Buggy trails follow the visible, deformed wing tips at each detail level. Missing or closed wings clear trail history so reopening them does not draw a stray connecting streak.
- Wingtip placement changes no vehicle handling. Jet trail anchors are unchanged.
