## Syn3h v26.11.1800

A Welcome guide, labelled lobby buttons, calmer lobby music and a longer opening.

Public release at 2:31pm Pacific on 18 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

These are design changes, shipped so the owner can adjust them on the live game. The lobby's round icons become labelled buttons in the lower right, Welcome, Updates and Settings, which open one frosted panel beneath the name. About is now Welcome everywhere, and opens a short illustrated guide to the game. The lobby's music trades its plucked string, bell and beeps for soft glass tones, and the opening takes its time, with the Syn3h name arriving last.

### Features

- Welcome, a short guide to Syn3h with pictures of the worlds and vehicles. It opens from the lobby's Welcome button and from the Welcome tab in play; its pictures load only when you open it.
- The lobby's controls are labelled buttons in the lower right, Welcome, Updates and Settings, each with its own icon. They open one frosted panel beneath the Syn3h name, which rises and shrinks above it, and switching between them changes the panel in place. Settings has moved into that panel. The version number moves to the lower left.
- The lobby's music is calmer: soft glass tones and shimmer in place of the plucked string, bell and beeps. In-world music is unchanged.
- The opening is longer, 8.6 seconds instead of 4.6, with the Syn3h name arriving last. A tap or a key skips it.

### Fixes

- A planet clicked while the opening is still bringing the planets in now enters the planet drawn under the pointer, not whichever one later rests there.
- Planet names orbit under the lobby's buttons instead of over them.
- Pressing Enter inside a lobby panel now reaches the control you are on.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/, watch the opening (or tap to skip it), then press Welcome for the guide.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1800, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

The Welcome guide's pictures load only when it is opened: about 450 KB on a desktop and 160 to 180 KB on a phone.

### Known issues

- These design changes are shipped for the owner to adjust on the live game.
- How the chat card and the keyboard behave on real phones is not yet confirmed.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
