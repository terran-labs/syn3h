## Mira v26.11.2401

A redrawn app icon, and the lobby name drawn as metal.

Public release at 1:30am Pacific on 24 November 2026, live at https://mira.cc/.

### The story so far

The owner approved a polished icon and a metal finish for the lobby name, to ship with the next production release tonight. Pages already open on an older version keep their own icon.

### Features

- A redrawn app icon: every ring and world now sits inside the square.
- The lobby name is drawn as metal lit by the sun. ?solid=0 gives the earlier flat look, ?tune=1 opens its tuning panel.
- If Mira is already on your home screen: on Android the new icon appears by itself within a day or two of opening it; on iPhone and iPad, remove the icon and add Mira to the Home Screen again.

### Fixes

No fixes in this release.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://mira.cc/, look at the lobby name, then add Mira to your home screen.

This exact version stays playable at https://mira.cc/?v=26.11.2401, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

The metal lobby name's cost was within measurement noise on a slowed phone (about 8 per cent more wall time in the builder's check).
