## Mira v26.11.2707

### Features

Settings → Light has a Headlights switch and a brightness slider. Both are saved on this device. Headlights start off.

### Fixes

The beam in mist uses the spotlight’s full outer angle and fades in at the lens. Light from nearby vehicles follows the terrain slope, with more detail close to the lamp.

### Handling

Driving and vehicle physics are unchanged.

### Play

Drive after dusk and adjust Headlight brightness to taste. The switch controls your lamps and the headlights you see on other vehicles.

### Performance

Switching headlights off skips drawing the mist beams and ground pools. The renderer keeps the local spotlights at zero intensity so toggling them can reuse the terrain shaders.

### Known issues

The buggy’s preferred beam reach still needs the owner’s judgment from the chase camera.
