## Syn3h v26.11.1608

No splash blur, a livelier buggy, calmer music and buggies that never stay boxes.

Public release at 3:53pm Pacific on 16 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

Driving into water or lava used to smear the whole screen; that effect is gone. The buggy had grown cautious over the weekend, so its new default turns in sharper at speed and settles itself when you let the wheel go, and every vehicle now has a Handling choice, including the buggy's Saturday feel. The score builds slowly over about five minutes and sound starts with your first touch. Some players saw their buggy stay a plain box for a whole session after one failed download; it now retries on its own. Switching vehicles carries on where you were, tag beams leave the vehicle that sends them, the minimap shows everyone in their own paint, and the public room keeps a clean address.

### Features

- The screen no longer blurs or tints when your vehicle touches or drives through water or lava.
- Handling: each vehicle's settings start with a Handling choice. Buggy: Balanced (the new default), Planted, Classic (Saturday's handling exactly), Direct and Assisted. Jet: Default, Gentle and Sharp. Hovercraft: Default, Gentle and Raw. Balanced turns in sharper at speed, settles itself when you let go of the steering, and does nothing to the buggy in the air.
- Music: the lobby has no theremin lead, the score takes about five minutes to build, and the lead is quieter by day and fuller at night.
- Sound starts on your first tap, click or key anywhere, including while the game loads; until then the Sound card says "Tap to start sound".
- The buggy's high-speed tyre squeal is now a softer scrub, with its own Tyre slip volume slider.
- Minimap: people are large arrows and bots small chevrons, each in their vehicle's own paint; vehicles beyond the map's edge show as outlined dots on the rim that fade with distance.
- Switching vehicles keeps you where you are, facing the same way and moving as you were. A switch in mid-air stays in the air, and an upside-down vehicle comes out upright.
- The address stays clean in the public room, and invite links and QR codes no longer carry a room name. Old links with the public room's name still work and are tidied. Private rooms keep their name in the link, and links to a pinned version stay pinned.
- Sun shafts are stronger by default.
- Tyre marks are soft and take their colour from the ground: a pale frosty scuff on ice and snow, a darker shade of the soil on grass and sand, faint on rock, fading at their edges and ends. Dust takes the colour and size of the surface, so there is no brown dust on ice, and the tank's tracks leave marks too.
- The Updates tab now lists the version you are playing, including this one.
- Syn3h's code stays under the PolyForm Noncommercial licence; its creative work (art, music and writing) is now under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

### Fixes

- If your buggy ever appeared as a plain box until you reloaded, that is fixed: a failed download now retries on its own.
- Closing the Zorb no longer flashes bright and hangs round the car; the shield only fades and shrinks.
- Tag beams now come from your vehicle, not from behind it. A Grapple cord seen on another player's vehicle also starts from where that vehicle is drawn.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ in a browser. Drag to look around the galaxy and tap a world to go there. Choose a Handling style at the top of your vehicle's settings in the sidebar, and change vehicle from the Me card at any speed. Invite friends from Players & session; the link opens your world in the public room.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1608, whatever ships after it.
