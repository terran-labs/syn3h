## Syn3h v26.11.1513

Vehicle switches gather in a cloud, pause for a second and drop, with new gear effects and sounds.

Public release at 4:05pm Pacific on 15 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

The switch hold and gear changes withdrawn with v26.11.1507 return after a quiet-machine comparison passed both the accepted control and withdrawn build twice. This release recomposes them with the current lobby, quality controls, water and vehicle fixes.

### Features

- Switching vehicles keeps your heading, clears speed and holds the new vehicle level for one second before it drops. Driving input and local beam launches and tag reports are ignored during the hold.
- Other players see a cosmetic arrival cloud when your vehicle kind changes. Their replica continues following your network snapshots.
- Switches have a gathering sound, held shimmer and release cue. Interrupted switches cancel the held sound; Zorb hops produce a pulse.
- Tag gains a thinner thread and impact rings, Plasma Thunder gathers inward motes, and Yoink surrounds a lifted vehicle with an amber field. Chaser launch and catch and the other gear actions have distinct generated cues.

### Fixes

- The Jet drops after a materialization hold instead of immediately hovering. Lift input can rearm its hover hold after settling.
- Rapid switches restore parked vehicles to dynamic physics and cancel the previous arrival effect cleanly.

### Play

[Play this exact release](https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1513). The plain address serves the latest accepted release.

### Known issues

- Hold protection is local. It does not guarantee immunity from another client's authoritative ram report.
- A remote Jet arrival briefly shifted vertically by 0.37 m in the browser check. Remote clouds are cosmetic and do not hold replica physics.
- The competitive bot-flee test still fails at the same result as the accepted baseline. The combined gate passed 1,401 tests with one skip and this one unchanged failure; no bounds were relaxed.
- The Jet's half-throttle default, camera-order change and throttle gauge are not included. The new quality governor and bot formation changes remain separate.
- The fallback showcase GIF comes from v26.11.1508 and predates recent vehicle and interface updates.

Validation: the frozen candidate passed normal and added-latency local multiplayer checks before deployment. Both public addresses passed exact-build, multiplayer and frozen-file checks; all eight Chrome/WebKit vehicle cases passed without page errors.
