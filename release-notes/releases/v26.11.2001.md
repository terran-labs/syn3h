## Syn3h v26.11.2001

Bots come and go without freezing the game, and loading is faster.

Public release at 2:00am Pacific on 20 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

The game used to stall for a moment whenever a bot joined, came back, left or came closer, because it was building the shaders that draw that vehicle and its effects in the middle of play. Now all of them are prepared when you press Play, behind the cover, all at once and starting during the dive. Bots arriving, leaving and changing distance no longer build shaders in play. Loading is faster than before, not slower.

### Features

- New: Copy diagnostics, under Performance, Diagnostics. It copies a private report of your device, startup timings, shader counts and any visible frame stalls. Nothing is sent anywhere; you choose where to paste it.

### Fixes

- Bots arriving, leaving and changing distance no longer build shaders in play, including repeats of the same vehicle.
- Every vehicle's and effect's shaders are prepared at Play, behind the cover, before the world appears.
- Bots share the drawing work they can while keeping their own paint, wheel glow and sparks.
- A name given in a link now shows on your own vehicle and in the Me card.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/, press Play, start a room with several bots and play while they come and go. Then open Performance, Diagnostics and choose Copy diagnostics.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.2001, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

From Play to playable is 0.6 to 3.3 s faster than v26.11.1906 by paired medians on the tested Mac. On both graphics backends, the first use of every vehicle and effect built no new shaders in play. A physical iPhone was not measured; touch was emulated on a Mac.

### Known issues

- Changing the quality level rebuilds 24 to 51 materials.
- Entering a world again repeats the preparation.
- The first touch on a phone builds one effect pipeline.
- On WebGL 2, preparation spends about 3 s waiting between completion checks.
- Not measured on a physical iPhone; touch was emulated on a Mac.
- The glass is a CSS imitation; a closer match to Apple's is in progress.
- Phone behaviour was tested in browser engines on a Mac, not on a physical iPhone.
- A player on an older version whose cord holds someone cannot be unhooked by the new stop words; stretching the cord still frees them.
- At dawn, fog lies on the sea along coasts.
- Bots' vehicles do not show headlight beams in mist yet.
- On dry ground, a vehicle left on its roof still rights itself after a few seconds; whether it should is the owner's to decide.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
