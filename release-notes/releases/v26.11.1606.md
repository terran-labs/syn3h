## Syn3h v26.11.1606

What's new inside the game, a settings gear in the lobby and loading lines worth reading.

Public release at 1:42pm Pacific on 16 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

Release notes used to live only on GitHub. Now About has an Updates tab, in the lobby and in play, with a short note for each release, and a slim banner under the wordmark says when a newer version is out. The lobby's Settings and Music panels have become one settings gear, with the running version beside it; click the version to see what's new. Loading screens now tell a few dry jokes instead of naming phases. Behind the scenes, two more connection fixes: a setting the host changes during a dropped connection is no longer lost, and a page that reconnects quickly always comes back as itself.

### Features

- Updates in About: the latest releases, newest first, each with what a player will notice and a link to the full notes. It is in the lobby's About and the in-play guide.
- Update banner: when a newer version is live, a slim line under the wordmark offers Reload and What's new. In play it is a small chip in the sidebar instead.
- Lobby settings gear: Sound (music and master volume, the rest under Advanced), Graphics, Controls (left-handed, look sensitivity, presentation mode) and Room (host lock and reset). Escape or a tap outside closes it; on a phone it is a sheet with its own close.
- The version beside the gear opens What's new.
- Loading lines: the loading screen rotates dry jokes, never the same one twice running. Screen readers still hear the plain loading step.

### Fixes

- A shared setting the host changes while their connection is down is now sent when they are back, instead of being undone by the room.
- A page that drops and reconnects within a moment always takes its own place back, even before the room has noticed the old connection closing.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ in a browser. Drag to look around the galaxy and tap a world to go there. The settings gear is in the lobby's lower right, with the version beside it; in play, What's new is in About under Players & session.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1606, whatever ships after it.

### Known issues
