## Syn3h v26.11.1901

Dawn fog banks, headlight beams in the mist, Plasma Tag strikes that spark and scorch, a buggy that skims water, and a sea you can't see through.

Public release at 3:00am Pacific on 19 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

Several worlds now wake to fog. At dawn, mist pools in valleys and lies along coasts, then burns off by mid-morning, and at night a buggy's headlights cut visible beams through it. Plasma Tag strikes are redrawn to look like real impacts. A buggy that goes under now sinks like it is in water, not honey, and at full throttle on the surface its tyres lift it to skim. Far hills no longer show through the sea, and the opening and the touch camera are tidier.

### Features

- At dawn, mist pools in valleys and lies along coasts on Alpine, the Archipelago, Ice and the swamp, and burns off by mid-morning. Drive into a bank and the distance greys. A setting, fog banks, turns it down or off.
- At night in mist, a buggy's headlights cut visible beams through the air: yours, other players' and bots'. A setting adjusts them or turns them off.
- Plasma Tag strikes look like impacts: a flash, sparks that scatter and bounce, a glow that fades, dust, steam or embers by surface, and a scorch that cools.
- Pinch with two fingers on the right of the screen to bring the chase camera in or out.

### Fixes

- Hills and spires beyond the horizon no longer show through the sea.
- A buggy that goes under sinks like it is in water, not honey; at full throttle on the surface its tyres lift it, so it skims faster and can climb out.
- The opening's point now sits exactly where the star bursts from; its text stays clear of the rings, and the rings are sharp.
- Spent particles are no longer drawn, and Plasma Tag strikes stay where they are drawn when you cross the world's seam.

### Handling

- A sunk buggy now sinks at about 3.3 m/s, down from a crawl at 1.3.
- From a 35 m/s entry, a buggy skimming at full throttle holds about 19 m/s, where it used to slow to a 5 m/s paddle.
- Opening the wings at the bottom of a pool brings a buggy to the surface a little faster.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ at dawn on the Archipelago, drive the buggy into the sea at full throttle, then wait for night and turn the headlights on in the mist.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1901, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Headlight beams are one extra draw, and are off at Minimal.

### Known issues

- At dawn, fog lies on the sea along coasts.
- Other vehicles do not have headlight beams yet.
- Very shallow ledges at a drop-off can read as water colour where they used to show sand.
- The pinch zoom was tested with simulated touches, not on a real iPhone.
- On dry ground, a vehicle left on its roof still rights itself after a few seconds; whether it should is the owner's to decide.
- Desert rivers stay a pale mint, from that world's palette.
- How the chat card and the keyboard behave on real phones is not yet confirmed.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
