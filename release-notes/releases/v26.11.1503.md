## Syn3h v26.11.1503

A planets-only lobby, a new interface and a generated hovercraft.

Public release at 11:20am Pacific on 15 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

**Play this exact version: https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1503**

That link always serves v26.11.1503 as it was frozen at 11:16am Pacific on 15 November
2026, before the noon deadline, however many releases follow it. The plain address,
https://www.syn3h.com/, serves the latest build.

### The story so far

The lobby used to ask for a vehicle and a world before play. It now shows only planets:
tap one and you land on it, and vehicles are chosen in play. The interface was rebuilt
around fine lines and panels that follow the cursor, and the game can now be paused. The
hovercraft is the first vehicle whose body is generated in code instead of loaded from a
model file. The physics engine moved into its own file, which cut the main script from
6.27 MB to 2.16 MB.

### Features

**Lobby**

The lobby is the galaxy and nothing else. Tap a planet to drop onto it. The Custom planet
is hidden for now. Planets no longer collide, labels respect depth, the sun's shafts are
soft, and dragging the view has a springy limit.

**Interface**

All four vehicles (Buggy, jet, hovercraft and tank) are in the in-play selector, with
colour swatches. Panels follow the cursor. Leaving a world asks for confirmation. Pressing
Escape frees the cursor; pressing it again pauses the game and fades the audio out.

**Vehicles**

The Buggy and the jet have new designs. The hovercraft is the generated Lowwing.

**Look**

The Syn3h logo lost its sun and gained a calmer glow. Water has a new look.

### Fixes

- The main script is 2.16 MB, down from 6.27 MB, because the physics engine ships as its
  own file.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1503 in a browser and tap a planet.

- Change vehicle and colour from the selector in play. In this version that reloads the page.
- Escape frees the cursor. A second Escape pauses.

### Known issues

- Switching vehicles reloads the page.
- The jet and the hovercraft show two small white glows floating ahead of the nose.
- The tank is still the earlier model.
- From just under the water at shallow angles, the sky looks too bright.
- Tag is the default mode. An Explore mode with no game running is not in this version.
- A frozen version plays on the live room server, with players on the latest build.
