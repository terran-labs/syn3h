## Mira v26.11.2306

A new Mira wordmark and app icon, and more ways to track the iPhone black flashes.

Public release at 9:00pm Pacific on 23 November 2026, live at https://mira.cc/.

### The story so far

The lobby gets the approved Mira wordmark, and the game a new app icon. For the black flashes on iPhone there are more test links, and every diagnostic link is now also a switch inside Performance, Diagnostics, so they can be tried without editing the address.

### Features

- A new Mira wordmark in the lobby and a new app icon.
- The diagnostic links are also switches inside Performance, Diagnostics, Field switches. They last for the visit and are never saved.
- More test links for the iPhone black flashes: ?frameCanary=1 counts frames the browser accepted and never drew; ?shadowCascades=1, ?shadowMap=512, ?shadowColor=0, ?shadowFilter=hard and ?shadowFade=0 change how sun shadows are drawn. On iPhone they need ?shadows=1 as well.

### Fixes

- If Mira is already on your home screen: on Android the new icon appears by itself within a day or two of opening it; on iPhone and iPad, remove the icon and add Mira to the Home Screen again.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://mira.cc/, look at the new wordmark, then add Mira to your home screen.

This exact version stays playable at https://mira.cc/?v=26.11.2306, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

No performance changes in this release; the links and switches change nothing unless used.

### Known issues

- The cause of the black flashes with sun shadows on is not yet fixed, only avoided by turning the shadows off.
- On the same devices water refraction, the force field contact glow and the headlight beams' soft edge are still off.
- The graphics process can still stop on iPhone.
- On an older page still open in a room, the room's gear looks replace that player's own.

- Quality changes to Ultra and Cinematic work as before.
- Under-water ripple needs WebGPU; on WebGL 2 the water looks as before.
- Its cost on a phone has not been measured; the copied diagnostics report says whether it is on.
- After the quality climbs from Low to Medium, a bot's shadow can build once.
- A Tag beam used under water still draws over the surface.
- Not yet tested in Safari with WebGPU.
- Which side of a sideways iPhone has the camera cutout is worked out from the screen's rotation and has not been checked on a real iPhone.
