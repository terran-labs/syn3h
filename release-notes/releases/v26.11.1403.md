### The story so far

The first buggy was judged not good enough to build on, and the direction changed that afternoon. The game's own Syn3h designs became the reference for vehicles, physics, interface and music wherever they differed from the older code it was ported from. The Syn3h buggy was ported from its own source, with its own colliders, mass, wheel positions, brakes and flight model, and became the default vehicle; the first port was kept as the classic buggy with its own body. In the same stretch the sky arrived with one sun direction driving the light, shadows, ambient and fog, the terrain gained graded roads, a lava lake, rivers and vents, and bots that chase you were ported along with the jet and hovercraft forces.

### Features

- The Syn3h buggy as the default, at full size, with force field wings and flight; the classic buggy as a second kind
- A live sky: sun, shadows, ambient light and fog all follow one sun direction, with sky presets
- Roads, a lava layout, vents and level of detail in the terrain, with more variety between seeds
- Bots that chase other players, spawned the same way on every machine
- Streamed music, positional sound effects, a start screen with player names, and touch controls

### Known issues

- The jet and hovercraft are ported but cannot yet be chosen
