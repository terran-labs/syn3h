## Mira v26.11.2502

A picture carousel in Welcome, Updates grouped by day, and a way to report a bug.

Public release at 9:00pm Pacific on 25 November 2026, live at https://mira.cc/.

### The story so far

The second beta. It brings the new Welcome carousel the owner reviewed, an Updates list that groups each day's releases, a Report a bug button, the creator's mark, and a round of small interface fixes.

### Features

- Welcome has a new picture carousel: the current capture in the centre with its neighbours angled beside it.
- Updates now shows one entry per day, with that day's releases and betas inside it.
- Report a bug, beside Screenshot on the pause screen, opens a short post for our Discord.
- The game's creator now carries a mark before their name.
- Anonymous counts now include the kind of device and graphics the game runs on, so we can tell which features most players can enjoy.
- A short note at the bottom of Welcome says what Mira counts: anonymous play and device counts and crash reports. No accounts, no ads, nothing sold.

### Fixes

- The gear list shows only what you can use, and its circles sit on their lines.
- The search field has its own clear button, and numbers that change in place no longer push the text beside them.
- The invite screen's Copy invite and Back are full-size buttons in one row.

### Handling

No handling changes in this release.

### Play

Open https://mira.cc/ and play as usual.

This exact version stays playable at https://mira.cc/?v=26.11.2502, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

Frame cost: not measured for v26.11.2501 (the machine was not quiet after it was accepted).

### Known issues

- While moving between worlds on a slow connection, the loading message can briefly change to a different line.

- The share card still draws the wordmark in the older dark style; the lobby look is being refined for a later release.

- The cause of the black flashes with sun shadows on is not yet fixed, only avoided by turning the shadows off.
- On the same devices water refraction, the force field contact glow and the headlight beams' soft edge are still off.
- The graphics process can still stop on iPhone.
- On an older page still open in a room, the room's gear looks replace that player's own.
- On a sideways phone the carousel's next picture passes under the Settings and Welcome buttons.

- Its cost on a phone has not been measured; the copied diagnostics report says whether it is on.

- After a climb to Ultra or Cinematic, a bot vehicle's mid-distance model can occasionally build once during play.

- Which side of a sideways iPhone has the camera cutout is worked out from the screen's rotation and has not been checked on a real iPhone.
