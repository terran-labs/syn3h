## Syn3h v26.11.2003

Plasma Thunder skips, flares and ends in fireworks.

Public release at 2:46am Pacific on 20 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

Plasma Thunder used to be a bolt that either connected or did not. Now it is a flare: it skips off the ground, goes off on contact with a vehicle or close to one, and every time it ends on the ground a flare climbs and bursts into fireworks that read across the map, with a ping on the minimap. Bots use it too, and their bolts follow the same rules through the host.

### Features

- Plasma Thunder skips off the ground up to three times, pushing what it passes a little at each skip.
- Contact with a vehicle sets it off and tags, as before. Passing close to a vehicle, once its fuse has armed, sets it off with a knock that never tags.
- Every ending on the ground sends a flare up about 120 m to burst into fireworks, and pings the spot on the minimap for 8 seconds.
- Six firework shapes in the player's colours: peony, chrysanthemum, ring, willow, palm and crossette. At night the stars are brighter and last longer, with glitter and a glow on the ground beneath. By day, smoke and wider stars carry the burst against the sky.
- The room decides where each bolt went off: the first valid report wins, and it must come from the player it touched, or from the host for a bot.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ at night, choose Plasma Thunder and send a bolt across the ground. Then try a close pass beside a bot.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.2003, whatever ships after it.

### Performance

- Phones draw fireworks with the lighter star and smoke counts. Six bursts at Low added about 1 ms of GPU time on a phone-sized screen.
- With more than two bursts alive, new ones draw with the lighter counts. Two Graphics settings tune this: "Firework bursts at full detail" and "Firework detail at High".
- On WebGPU, the first burst built no new pipelines or shader modules, at night and at noon. Six bursts at High on desktop did not change frame time in our measurement.

### Known issues

- Changing the quality level in play rebuilds the firework batches.
- On the older WebGL 2 graphics path, the number of shaders the first burst builds is not measured.
- A player still on the previous version sees Plasma Thunder as it was and ignores the new messages. Their version never reports where its bolts end, so players on this version burst those bolts on their own after 1.5 s, and those bursts push nobody.
- Changing the quality level rebuilds 24 to 51 materials.
- Entering a world again repeats the preparation.
- The first touch on a phone builds one effect pipeline.
- On WebGL 2, preparation spends about 3 s waiting between completion checks.
- Not measured on a physical iPhone; touch was emulated on a Mac.
- The glass is a CSS imitation; a closer match to Apple's is in progress.
- Phone behaviour was tested in browser engines on a Mac, not on a physical iPhone.
- A player on an older version whose cord holds someone cannot be unhooked by the new stop words; stretching the cord still frees them.
- At dawn, fog lies on the sea along coasts.
- Bots' vehicles do not show headlight beams in mist yet.
- On dry ground, a vehicle left on its roof still rights itself after a few seconds; whether it should is the owner's to decide.
- Sound on a real iPhone in silent mode is unconfirmed until the owner tries it.
- Chat's word filter is short and English only.
