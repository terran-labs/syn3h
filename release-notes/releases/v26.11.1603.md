## Syn3h v26.11.1603

A one-line command bar, a thrust readout for the Jet and bots that really run away in Tag.

Public release at 10:32am Pacific on 16 November 2026, live at https://www.syn3h.com/.

### The story so far

Search and Pause used to sit at the bottom of the sidebar. They now share one line at the top with the world name, and on a phone the search field simply says Search so nothing is cut off. The Jet's focus display now shows how hard the engine is pushing. Behind the scenes, three tests that had been failing on every run were fixed or retired. One of them was hiding a real bug: in competitive Tag, bots meant to flee were driving in tight circles instead.

### Features

- The world name, Search and Pause sit on one line above the sidebar cards, on desktop and phone.
- Flying the Jet, the focus display shows THRUST as a percentage under the height above ground. Other vehicles do not show it.

### Fixes

- In Tag with following turned off, bots that are not it now run away from whoever is it, and the bot that is it hunts properly. Before, a fleeing buggy often drove in small circles a few metres from where it started.
- Bots following a route across open ground no longer swing round their own aim point at full lock.

### Play

Open https://www.syn3h.com/ in a browser. Drag to look around the galaxy and tap a world to go there. In play, open the sidebar: the command bar is at the top, and Search finds any setting. To fly the Jet, open the Me card and pick Jet from the Vehicle menu. Raise and lower thrust with R and F, or THR + and THR − on a phone, and watch the readout.

This exact version stays playable at https://www.syn3h.com/?v=26.11.1603, whatever ships after it.
