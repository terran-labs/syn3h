A driving column, touch aiming and chat in the panel

- The left of the driving HUD is now one column: gear, switches, chat, map.
- On a phone, drag TAG to aim it like a second stick.
- Chat is now a card at the bottom of the right panel; Y opens it.
- The minimap turns with you, heading up. A setting keeps north up.
- Pick gear with number keys or a tap; T no longer cycles gear.
