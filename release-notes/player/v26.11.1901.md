Dawn fog, headlight beams and sparking Plasma Tag

- At dawn, mist pools in valleys and lies along the coasts.
- At night, a buggy's headlights cut beams through the mist.
- Plasma Tag strikes flash, spark, glow and leave a scorch.
- A sunk buggy sinks like it is in water, and skims at full throttle.
- Far hills no longer show through the sea.
