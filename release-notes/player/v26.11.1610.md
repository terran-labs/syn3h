A new galaxy, real water and lava, steadier labels

- The lobby's planets are painted from their own worlds, labelled beside them.
- Buggies float and paddle in water, ride through lava, and steer while afloat.
- Seas, lakes and lava show real waves, foam, glow, and ice on the Ice Moon.
- Vehicle labels stay put, are never hidden by your own car, and read far off.
- The Zorb bounces, and holding brake makes a landing stick.
