A new vehicle switch, plus new gear effects and sounds

- Switching vehicles gathers a cloud, holds the new vehicle for a second, then drops it facing the same way.
- Other players see the arrival cloud when you switch.
- Switching and Zorb hops have their own sounds.
- Tag, Plasma Thunder, Yoink and the Chaser have new effects and sounds.
- After a switch the Jet drops to the ground instead of hovering straight away.
