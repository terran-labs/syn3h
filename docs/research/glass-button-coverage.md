# Liquid button coverage

| Location | Shader surfaces |
| --- | --- |
| Driving | The gear selector and its action buttons, accessory and chat buttons, Show panels, the touch actions for the current vehicle, and the three pause actions. Brake and Zorb keep their shared outline. |
| Panel rail | Search, Pause, Leave world, Close panels, the update chip and each panel card. Opening a card moves its surface with the painted outline. |
| Galaxy | Settings, Welcome and Updates where present, including the dock beside an open sheet. The Settings card owns one surface. On the unplated Welcome and Updates sheets the standalone buttons, including Close, draw over the galaxy. |

## Controls that stay plain

* Planet captions and the version text stay unplated by the owner's choice. A saved moon represents the world itself and keeps its procedural artwork.
* The Settings card, gameplay cards, race status, performance recommendation, update banner, confirmation prompts and invite view contain their own controls. Their rows, tabs, switches, close buttons, copy actions, reset buttons and text fields keep one input target apiece. They share their containing surface.
* The Play form and world settings have a panel behind their fields and buttons. The in-game Welcome dialog and chat compose view also have their own backing.
* A media button containing a picture or video keeps the media visible. Links in articles and release notes remain text.
* Tap to begin and the paused screen's click-to-resume target cover the viewport. Their labels remain visible without turning the entire scene into a lens.
* The minimap displays terrain and positions. The floating steering stick reports the player's thumb position. Neither is a glass button.
