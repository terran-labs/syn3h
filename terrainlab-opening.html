The opening view on each planet

What a player sees in the first second, taken with the game's own camera at spawn pad 0. Planets are drawn about 1.4 times their true radius (roundness 0.71; true means the sphere with the same surface area as the world), so the centre's mountain stands clear of the horizon, and every world wraps: drive straight and you come home.
